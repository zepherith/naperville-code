# 4-9 SMOKE FREE

SECTION:\
4-9-1: Adoption Of Smoke Free Illinois Act\
4-9-2: Enforcement

### 4-9-1: ADOPTION OF SMOKE FREE ILLINOIS ACT

1\. The Smoke Free Illinois Act (Public Act 095-0017), as amended from time to time, is hereby adopted by reference to be applied within the corporate limits of the City of NapeNille to the extent permitted by law and to the extent that its subject matter is not regulated by this Code or inconsistent with any lawful provisions of this Code.

2\. All references in sections of the Smoke Free Illinois Act to this "Act" or "Code" shall be to sections of the Smoke Free Illinois Act. References to a State employee or official may be to a corresponding City employee or official where such reference is applicable or necessary. Any provision of the Smoke Free Ill inois Act adopted herein may be cited by placing "4-9 :" before the appropriate Smoke Free Illinois Act section, for example, 4-9 :1-123, NapeNille Municipal Code.

3\. The use of any "alternative nicotine product" as defined under 720 Illinois Compiled Statutes 675/1.5(a), as amended from time to time, specifically the use of what is commonly known as electronic cigarettes or e-cigarettes, shall be included within the definition of "smoking" or "smoke" under the Smoke Free Illinois Act, 410 Illinois Compiled Statutes 82/10, as amended from time to time. (Ord. No. 07-252, 12-4-2007; Ord. No. 15-126, § 2, 7-21-2015)

### 4-9-2: ENFORCEMENT

1\. The Police Department and Code Enforcement Division of the Department of Transportation, Engineering and Development are empowered to adopt procedures for the issuance of citations and the collection of civil penalties and costs under this Chapter, to the extent that they do not contradict or are otherwise inconsistent with the provisions of this Code. (Ord. 07-252, 12-4-2007)
