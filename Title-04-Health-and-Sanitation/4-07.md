As used in this Section, the following words shall have the following meanings:

### 4-7-2: RECORDS TO BE MAINTAINED

Each body art establishment shall maintain permanent records for each patron. Such records shall be provided within the body art establishment and shall be available for examination by the DuPage County Health Department or Will County Health Department, whichever is applicable. Such records shall be maintained by the body art establishment for a period of at least two (2) years.

(Ord. 99-181, 10-19-1999; Ord. No. 19-099, § 2, 8-20-2019)

### 4-7-3: INFECTIONS TO BE REPORTED

No patron having any skin infection or other disease of the skin or any communicable disease whatsoever shall be tattooed. All infections resulting from the practice of tattooing which become known to the tattoo establishment shall promptly be reported to the DuPage County Health Department or Will County Health Department, whichever is applicable.

(Ord. 99-181, 10-19-1999)

### 4-7-4: PROHIBITIONS

1\. Any person under the age of eighteen (18) years shall be prohibited from receiving the services of body art establishments unless such person is accompanied by a parent or legal guardian who authorizes, in writing, such body art.

2\. It is prohibited for any operator or employee of the body art establishments to tattoo or pierce any person under the influence of alcohol, drugs or a controlled substance.

(Ord. 99-181, 10-19-1999; Ord. No. 14-018, § 2, 3-4-2014; Ord. No. 19-080, § 2, 6-4-2019; Ord. No. 19-099, § 2, 8-20-2019)

### 4-7-5: VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES

Any person, firm or corporation in violation of any provision of this Chapter shall be deemed guilty of a misdemeanor and, upon conviction thereof, shall be fined not less than fifty dollars (\$50.00) nor more than five hundred dollars (\$500.00) for each offense. A separate offense shall be deemed to be committed for each person and on each day on which a violation occurs or continues.

(Ord. 99-181, 10-19-1999)

### 4-7-6: ZONING ALLOWANCES

Body art establishments shall be located in compliance with the requirements of Title 6 (Zoning Ordinance). Body art establishments shall be permitted as accessory to a permitted beauty shop/salon, health spa without lodging accommodations/massage establishments, and/or medical office/clinic provided that the area in which the body art services are provided shall not exceed twenty-five percent (25%) of the gross floor area of the principal permitted use.

(Ord. No. 19-099, § 2, 8-20-2019)
