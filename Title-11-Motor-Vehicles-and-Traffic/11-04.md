# 11-4 BICYCLES, SKATEBOARDS, ROLLER SKATES, IN-LINE SKATES, E-BIKES, E-SCOOTERS, AND OTHER MOBILITY DEVICES[^4]

### 11-4-1: DEFINITIONS

Whenever in this Chapter the following terms are used, they shall have the meanings respectively ascribed to them in this Section:

| BICYCLE:       | Every device propelled by human power upon which any person may ride, having two (2) tandem wheels except scooters and similar devices.                                                                                                                 |
|----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| RENTAL AGENCY: | Any person engaged in the business of offering for rental and renting bicycles for hire by the public in the City.                                                                                                                                      |
| ROLLER SKATES: | Any device worn on the foot with wheels and which is propelled by human power. This definition includes devices commonly known as in-line skates.                                                                                                       |
| SERIAL NUMBER: | An identifying number permanently stamped or cast on a bicycle's frame.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| SKATEBOARD:    | A device with two (2) or more tandem axles and a combination of wheels connected by a platform designed for standing and which is propelled by human power. (Ord. No. 92-56, 4-7-1992; amd. Ord. No. 94-166, 9-6-1994; Ord. No. 22-124, § 1, 12-6-2022) |

### 11-4-3: LIMITATIONS; BICYCLES, SKATEBOARDS, ROLLER SKATES AND IN-LINE SKATES

1\. The rules for riding and operating bicycles shall be as set forth in provisions of the Illinois Vehicle Code pertaining to bicycles adopted by reference by Section 11-1-1 of this Code unless otherwise provided herein.

2\. No bicycles shall be operated upon any sidewalk in the Central Business District or shopping center within the City.

3\. No skateboards, in-line roller skates or roller skates shall be used, operated, or propelled upon any sidewalk or public way in the Central Business District or shopping center of the City, the Naperville Riverwalk, parks under the jurisdiction of the City, any property owned or operated by the City, including commuter train stations, or on property owned by the public library.

4\. The Director of the Department of Public Works of the City shall post official traffic control devices prohibiting such operations in accordance with Section 11-1:11-1512 of this Code.

5\. No skateboards, in-line skates or roller skates shall be used, operated or propelled upon any private property where there is a written agreement to such effect between the City and the property owner.

6\. Owner(s) of private property having a written enforcement agreement with the City shall post appropriate signs prohibiting such operation, which signs shall be subject to approval by the Director of the Department of Public Works.

7\. Each violation of this Section of the City Municipal Code shall be punishable by a fine of not less than seventy-five dollars (\$75.00).

8\. The following persons shall be exempt from the provisions of this Section while in the discharge of their duties:

8.1. Peace officers;

8.2. City of Naperville Community Service Officers (CSO).

(Ord. No. 95-130, 6-20-1995; Ord. No. 96-174, 10-1-1996; Ord. 94-166, 9-6-1994)

### 11-4-4: RENTAL AGENCIES

1\. Equipment: Bicycles offered for rent by a rental agency shall be equipped by the rental agency with the lights and other equipment required by this Chapter and shall be kept in good and safe working condition. Rental agencies shall comply with all the provisions of this Chapter, and shall be responsible for the condition of the bicycle they rent.

(Ord. No. 83-53, 5-16-1983; Ord. No. 22-124, § 1, 12-6-2022)

### 11-4-5: DEALERS

1\. Report: Any person engaged in the business of selling, buying or trading new or used bicycles within the City is hereby required to make a written report to the Chief of Police at the end of each month, or more often if requested by them. Such report shall contain the following information:

1.1. The name and address of each person to whom and from whom a bicycle was sold, purchased or traded during the period covered by the report, not including, however, purchases of new bicycles from regularly established jobbers or wholesalers.

1.2. A description of the bicycle involved in each such transaction consisting of the name of the manufacturer and the serial number of the frame.

(Ord. No. 83-53, 5-16-1983; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022; Ord. No. 22-124, § 1, 12-6-2022)

### 11-4-6: OTHER PROVISIONS

1\. Removal Of Serial Number: It shall be unlawful for any person willfully or maliciously to remove, destroy, mutilate or alter the serial number of any bicycle. The Chief of Police may authorize the stamping of numbers on the frame of a bicycle in a legible manner, for identification purposes, upon which no serial number can be found, or upon which the serial number is illegible or insufficient for identification purposes.

2\. Use Of Bicycle Without Consent Of Owner: It shall be unlawful for any person to use or operate any bicycle within the City without the consent of the owner of the bicycle.

3\. Bicycle Without Serial Number: It shall be unlawful for any person to purchase, receive in trade, or otherwise acquire any bicycle from which the serial number on the frame has been removed, destroyed, mutilated or altered, without first reporting the same to the Police Department.

(Ord. No. 83-53, 5-16-1983; Ord. No. 92-56, 4-7-1992; Ord. No. 22-124, § 1, 12-6-2022)

### 11-4-7-2: CLASSIFICATION AND EQUIPMENT

1\. E-bikes operated within the City of Naperville must comply with Federal Consumer Product Safety Commission standards and Class 1 E-Bikes, Class 2 E-Bikes, and Class 3 E-Bikes must bear a permanent visible label indicating its class, motor wattage, and top assisted speed. Modification of an e-bike to exceed its classified speed or power limits shall result in reclassification or prohibition under this ordinance.

2\. E-bikes and e-scooters operated within the City of Naperville must well-maintained and in good operating condition and must conform to the regulations consistent with the Illinois Vehicle Code governing their operation, including being equipped with:

2.1. Brakes that will adequately control and stop the movement of the device, and that at a minimum are capable of stopping the device within thirty (30) feet from fifteen (15) mph on dry, level pavement;

2.2. A front-facing white light and rear facing red reflector or light visible from five hundred (500) feet during hours of darkness (sunset to sunrise) or low visibility;

2.3. For e-bikes, reflective tires or wheel rims; and

2.4. For Class 3 E-Bikes, a functional speedometer.

(Ord. No. 25-133, § 2, 12-3-2025)

### 11-4-7-3: OPERATION AND USE

1\. General Rights and Duties: Except as specified in this ordinance, e-bike and e-scooter operators shall have the same rights and duties as operators of traditional bicycles under the Illinois Vehicle Code (e.g., 625 ILCS 5/11-1502), including yielding at stop signs, signaling turns, and riding with traffic.

2\. Age Restrictions:

2.1. E-bike operators, including operators of Class 1 E-Bikes, Class 2 E-Bikes, and Class 3 E-Bikes, must be at least sixteen (16) years of age and must be able to safely operate the device.

2.2. E-scooter operators must be at least eighteen (18) years of age, consistent with 625 ILCS 5/11-1518(c), and must be able to safely operate the device.

3\. Where E-Bikes and E-Scooters May Be Operated:

3.1. Roadways: E-bikes and e-scooters may be operated on any street or roadway where bicycles are permitted, subject to all traffic laws applicable to bicycles under the Illinois Vehicle Code and the Naperville Municipal Code. However, e-scooters may not be operated on a highway with a posted speed limit in excess of thirty-five (35) mph, consistent with 625 ILCS 5/11-1518(b), or on State highways, consistent with 625 ILCS 5/11-1518(k).

3.2. Bicycle Lanes: Class 1 E-Bikes, Class 2 E-Bikes, and E-Scooters are permitted in bicycle lanes within the City of Naperville. Class 3 E-Bikes are permitted in bicycle lanes only if the bicycle lane is designed for speeds up to twenty-eight (28) mph or local signage allows.

3.3. Multi-Use Paths: E-bikes, Class 1 E-Bikes, Class 2 E-Bikes, and e-scooters are permitted on a multi-use path unless prohibited or otherwise regulated by the City of Naperville or unless prohibited or otherwise regulated by another managing government authority such as the Forest Preserve District of DuPage County, the Forest Preserve District of Will County, or the Naperville Park District (see Naperville Park District Ordinance 641). Class 3 E-Bikes are prohibited on a multi-use path in the City of Naperville unless explicitly authorized by the City of Naperville or by another managing government authority.

3.4. Sidewalks: E-bikes and e-scooters shall not be operated on Sidewalks within the City of Naperville, except where expressly permitted by official City of Naperville signage or when necessary to enter or exit adjacent private property.

3.5. Prohibited Areas: E-bikes and e-scooters are prohibited on highways, expressways, State highways, the Riverwalk, or any area posted as off-limits to bicycles in the City of Naperville. E-scooters are also prohibited throughout the entire park system of the Naperville Park District.

4\. Administrative Authority: The City of Naperville's City Manager or his or her designee may authorize the posting of regulations restricting the use of devices defined in this ordinance within the City of Naperville when deemed necessary, in the sole discretion of the City Manager or his or her designee, for public safety, preservation of property, or consistency with adopted City plans.

5\. Speed Limits: E-bike and e-scooter operators shall not exceed posted speed limits. On multi-use paths, e-bikes and e-scooters shall not exceed a maximum speed of fifteen (15) mph.

6\. Helmet Requirement: All e-bike, e-scooter operators and passengers are encouraged to wear an ANSI, SNELL, or CPSC-approved helmet when operating or riding e-bikes or e-scooters.

(Ord. No. 25-133, § 2, 12-3-2025)

### 11-4-7-4: PROHIBITED ACTS

For the purposes of this ordinance, the following acts are prohibited in the City of Naperville:

1\. Operating a personal mobility device, e-motor, modified e-bike, or modified e-scooter on public property.

2\. Reckless operation of an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor, including disobeying traffic regulations and weaving through traffic.

3\. Operating an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor while wearing headphones or otherwise obstructing the operator's hearing.

4\. Operating an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor while impaired, including under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

5\. Operating an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor equipped with a siren or oscillating red and blue lights.

6\. Operating an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor while attached, by person or device, to any other vehicle being operated on a public right-of-way.

7\. Operating an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor while carrying any package, bundle, or article that prevents the operator from keeping at least one (1) hand upon the device's handlebars or steering controls.

8\. Operating an e-bike or e-scooter to transport more than one (1) passenger at a time, or operating an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor to transport a passenger if the device is not designed and equipped for multiple riders.

9\. Operating an e-bike, e-scooter, personal mobility device, or e-motor while obstructing pedestrians or failing to yield to pedestrians.

10\. Tampering with or removing the class label on an e-bike or e-scooter or operating an e-bike without a label clearly identifying the accurate e-bike class.

(Ord. No. 25-133, § 2, 12-3-2025)

### 11-4-7-5: PARKING AND STORAGE

1\. E-bikes and e-scooters may be parked in bicycle racks or designated bicycle parking areas.

2\. No e-bike or e-scooter shall be parked in a manner that obstructs pedestrian pathways, Americans with Disabilities Act accessibility, or emergency access routes.

(Ord. No. 25-133, § 2, 12-3-2025)

### 11-4-7-6: PARENTAL AND GUARDIAN RESPONSIBILITY

1\. Parents and guardians of minors are responsible for the appropriate operation of e-bikes, e-scooters, personal mobility devices, and e-motors owned by said parents and guardians or owned by minors under their direct control.

2\. Any violation of this ordinance committed by a minor under the direct control of, or with the consent of, their parent or guardian shall subject said parent or guardian to a violation of this ordinance and the penalties provided in this ordinance.

3\. The City of Naperville shall have the discretion and authority to impound e-bikes, e-scooters, personal mobility devices, e-motors, or other devices regulated under this ordinance for noncompliance with this ordinance. When such a device so impounded is owned by a minor, the City of Naperville shall have the discretion and authority to release such impounded device to the minor's parent or guardian.

(Ord. No. 25-133, § 2, 12-3-2025)

### 11-4-7-7: ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES

1\. Violations of this ordinance may be enforced by the City of Naperville Police Department and the City of Naperville's Code Enforcement.

2\. For any violation of this ordinance, the minimum penalty will be not less than one hundred dollars (\$100.00) nor more than five hundred dollars (\$500.00) for each offense, and each day a violation continues shall constitute a separate offense.

3\. The City of Naperville shall have the discretion and authority to impound e-bikes, e-scooters, personal mobility devices, e-motors, or other devices regulated under this ordinance when it is determined to be appropriate to ensure compliance with this ordinance.

(Ord. No. 25-133, § 2, 12-3-2025)

### 11-4-7-8: EXCEPTIONS

This ordinance shall not apply to:

1\. E-bikes, e-scooters, personal mobility devices, or e-motors when operated by authorized public safety personnel in the course and scope of their duties; and

2\. Personal assistance mobility devices or other mobility devices when appropriately operated in accordance with authorization under federal, state, or local law as necessary to provide a reasonable disability accommodation.

(Ord. No. 25-133, § 2, 12-3-2025)
