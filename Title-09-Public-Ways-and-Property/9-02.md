# 9-2 STREET NUMBERING

### 9-2-1: DESIGNATION OF STREETS AND AVENUES

All roadways in the City running easterly and westerly in direction shall be designated as avenues or roads, and all roadways running northerly and southerly in direction shall be designated as streets and drives, with cul-de-sacs designated as courts and curvilinear roadways with no prevalent direction as lanes, as near as shall be practicable.

Curved roadways which are continuous and which begin and end with two (2) separate intersections with the same collector roadway, shall be designated as circles, which designation shall be reserved for this type of roadway and not used for a cul-de-sac or other.

### 9-2-2: NUMBERING SYSTEM

All lots, buildings and structures in the City shall be numbered in accordance with the following plan as near as shall be practicable:

1\. The base line for streets running north and south shall be Benton Avenue, and numbers lying north or south shall be designated north or south as the case may be; the base line for avenues running east and west shall be Washington Street, and numbers lying east and west of this line shall be designated east or west as the case may be. The street direction from the base line is required only for roadways that cross a base line. Numbering shall begin with the base lines with the number one, and one unit shall be allowed for each twenty (20) feet of frontage; provided, that the numbers at each block shall begin with one hundred (100) or a multiple thereof.

2\. Even numbers shall be on the east and south sides of the streets. Odd numbers shall be on the north and west sides of the avenues.

3\. Street numbering of circles shall be consecutive beginning at the end closest to the appropriate base line, with even numbers on the inside of the circle and odd numbers on the outside.

4\. Each additional building on a single lot will have a unique building address. Subdivisions of a building will be designated suites and use at least a three-digit numeric format. The first digit(s) will indicate floor of building and the last two (2) digits the suite. Suite numbering shall allow one unit for each ten (10) feet of frontage to public access areas.

5\. All street numbers shall be assigned by the Director of the Department of Public Works, or their designate. To guard against the misdirection of emergency personnel of the delay in emergency response, no one shall utilize any other designation for their property on any sign, street graphic or communication other than the official street number and street name assigned by the Department of Public Works.

(Ord. No. 89-64, 4-3-89; amd. Ord. No. 92-137, 7-21-92; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 9-2-3: BUILDING NUMBERS REQUIRED

It shall be the duty of the owner, managing agent and occupant of every building in the City to have placed thereon, in a place easily visible from the street, figures at least five and one-half (5½) inches high, and of contrasting color to the building's exterior, showing the street address number of the building.

1\. For any multiple dwelling, residential building or any office, commercial or other nonresidential building in the City, there shall have the additional requirement place thereon, in places easily visible from both front and rear outside accesses, figures showing the street address numbers and suite numbers of units contained therein.

2\. For the purposes of complying with paragraph 1, such figures shall not be placed on doors of the structures or in any place on the building where it would not be visible if the door were open.

3\. No temporary or final occupancy permit shall be issued for any building in the City described in paragraphs 1 or 2 hereof unless the owner, occupant or managing agent thereof has complied with the requirements hereof.

(Ord. No. 89-64, 4-3-89)

### 9-2-4: STANDARDS ON FILE

The Director of the Department of Public Works shall compile and maintain standards for naming and addressing public streets, which standards shall be open to inspection by anyone interested.

1\. At a minimum, the street address standard shall include:

1.1. Street address number base map extended to the approved City planning boundary.

1.2. Guidelines for naming streets and numbering lots, building and structures.

1.3. Procedures for proposing changes to street names or address in the event duplicate names or numbers are discovered.

(Ord. No. 89-64, 4-3-89; amd. Ord. No. 92-137, 7-21-92)
