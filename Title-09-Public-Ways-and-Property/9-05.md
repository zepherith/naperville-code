# 9-5 CITY PARKS

SECTION:\
9-5-1: Riverwalk Park Regulations

### 9-5-1: RIVEWALK PARK REGULATIONS

1\. The Guidelines for Riverwalk Use, heretofore approved by the City Council, are hereby adopted by reference in their entirety and made a part hereof. Such Guidelines shall be and remain on file in the office of the City Clerk.

2\. The Riverwalk Park shall be closed to the public from twelve o'clock (12:00) midnight to six o'clock (6:00) a.m. every day of the week. No person shall remain in the Riverwalk Park when it is not open to the public.

3\. All performances in and upon Riverwalk Park must first be authorized by a permit from the City Manager specifying time, location and details of said performance in accordance with the Guidelines adopted in Subsection 1 of this Section.

4\. No person shall engage in any team sport or game such as, but not limited to, baseball, football, field hockey, Frisbee throwing, volleyball or horseshoes, at any time in or upon the Riverwalk Park.

5\. No person shall, in or upon Riverwalk Park:

5.1. Cut, remove, uproot or wantonly destroy any tree, sapling, seedling, bush, shrub, flower or plant whether alive or dead or chip, blaze, box, girdle, trim or otherwise deface or injure any tree, shrub or bush, break or remove any branch or foliage thereof or pick or gather any seed of any tree or plant unless necessitated by the performance of restorative, maintenance or construction work pursuant to contract with the City.

5.2. Remove or cause to be removed any sod, earth, humus, downed timber, wood chips, peat, rock, sand or gravel or remove or cause to be removed any other natural material of the forest floor or earth unless necessitated by the performance of restorative, maintenance, or construction work pursuant to contract with the City.

5.3. Hunt, pursue, trap, catch, capture, molest, poison, wound or kill or attempt to hunt, trap, catch, capture, molest, poison, wound or kill any invertebrate animal, mammal, bird, reptile or amphibian; disturb, molest or rob the nest of any mammal, bird, insect, reptile or amph ibian or the lair, den or burrow of any mammal, bird, reptile or amphibian. (Ord. 84-110, 8-20-84)
