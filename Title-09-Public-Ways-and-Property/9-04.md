# 9-4 BLOCK PARTIES

### 9-4-1: PERMIT REQUIRED

It shall be unlawful to conduct or attend a block party or any other social gathering of persons which disrupts or blocks pedestrian or vehicular traffic on any public right-of-way unless a permit has been issued for such block party or gathering in accordance with this Chapter.

(Ord. No. 76-43, 5-17-1976; Ord. No. 15-035, § 1, 3-17-2015)

### 9-4-2: APPLICATION AND FEE

Any person desirous of securing the permit required by Section 9-4-1 of this Chapter shall file an application with the City Clerk upon a form provided by them, setting forth the following information:

1\. Proposed date of event and alternate date in case of rain.

2\. Location of right-of-way to be affected by proposed event.

3\. Hours during which event is proposed to be held.

4\. Name, address and telephone number of person over the age of eighteen (18) years to be designated as sponsor of proposed event.

5\. Whether or not an amplifier will be used.

6\. Signatures of one adult resident from seventy-five percent (75%) of the households whose residences abut that portion of the public right-of-way to be affected by the proposed event.

Applications shall be submitted, along with a nonrefundable review fee of ten dollars (\$10.00) not less than fourteen (14) days prior to the proposed date of the event.

(Ord. No. 76-43, 5-17-1976; Ord. No. 15-035, § 2, 3-17-2015; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 9-4-3: REVIEW OF APPLICATION; ISSUANCE OF PERMIT

Applications shall be reviewed by the TED Business Group, and the report from said Department shall be forwarded to the City Clerk for review and determination of whether such a permit shall be issued. If the application complies with the provisions of this Chapter, the City Clerk shall issue the required permit unless:

1\. The proposed block party or gathering is to be located on any public right-of-way other than a "Local Street" as defined by the City Master Thoroughfare Plan; or

2\. One of the City departments reviewing the application has reported that the effect of the proposed block party or gathering will endanger the public health, welfare and safety or will unduly interfere with the flow of traffic.

3\. Fifteen (15) permits have been issued for the weekend of the request. Applications are processed in order they are received.

(Ord. No. 76-43, 5-17-1976; amd. 1984 Code; Ord. No. 92-137, 7-21-1992; Ord. No. 03-13, 1-21-2003; Ord. No. 15-035, § 3, 3-17-2015)

### 9-4-4: RESTRICTIONS AND EXEMPTIONS

Any permit issued pursuant to this Chapter shall be subject to the following restrictions and exemptions:

1\. No block party or gathering shall extend beyond twelve o'clock (12:00) midnight upon any portion of the public right-of-way.

2\. No public right-of-way shall be completely blocked by any block party or gathering, but rather, access must be available for any person wishing to use the public right-of-way for either pedestrian or vehicular purposes.

3\. That portion of the public right-of-way to be used by the block party or gathering shall be delineated by the appropriate number of barricades provided by the City. The sponsor of the block party or gathering shall be required to sign a statement of responsibility for such barricades. Barricades will be delivered and picked up from the sponsor's residence by the City. The sponsor shall be responsible for placing the barricades upon the public right-of-way in accordance with City instructions. In the event the barricades are not available for pick up by the City at the sponsor's residence, the City Clerk will bill the party sponsor at the rate of one hundred dollars (\$100.00) per barricade.

4\. No block party or gathering shall occupy more than six hundred (600) linear feet of any public right-of-way.

5\. No alcoholic liquor shall be sold at any block party or gathering.

6\. The sponsor shall be responsible for the removal of all litter, refuse or property deposited in the public right-of-way as a result of the block party or gathering at the end of such block party or gathering.

7\. No person attending any block party or gathering in the public right-of-way shall make any loud noise or disturbance which will create a nuisance for persons not attending the block party or gathering.

8\. The sponsor will be responsible for any costs incurred by the City for establishing detours, assigning of police officers for traffic direction, clean up or repair of public right-of-way used or damaged by block party or gathering. The sponsor shall be required to sign a statement as to such responsibility at the time a permit is issued.

9\. No portion of the public right-of-way for which a block party permit has been issued shall be considered as a street, highway, public sidewalk or public place for purposes of Subsection 10-2-1-2.2, and Section 10-2-2-2 of this Code for the duration of such permit.

10\. No person shall maintain or operate any loudspeaker or amplifier connected with any radio, microphone or other device by which sounds are magnified and made heard over any public street or public place in the City without first securing an amplifier permit in accordance with the provisions of Title 3, Chapter 9 of this Code.

(Ord. No. 76-43, 5-17-1976; Ord. No. 85-187, 10-7-1985; Ord. No. 03-13, 1-21-2003; amd. Ord. No. 08-086, 5-6-2008; Ord. No. 15-035, § 4, 3-17-2015)
