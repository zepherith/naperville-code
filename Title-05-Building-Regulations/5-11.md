### 5-11-1: TITLE

This Chapter shall be entitled CONDOMINIUM REGULATIONS OF THE CITY OF NAPERVILLE. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)

### 5-11-2: APPLICABILITY

This Chapter applies to all multiple-family dwellings and commercial condominiums containing more than tour (4) dwelling units located on any one parcel or lot within the City which are to be developed as new or as a condominium conversion. No partial conversion of any one building to condominium status shall be permitted. This Chapter shall apply to "add on condominiums" as defined herein. This Chapter shall not apply to the resale of any condominium unit.

1\. No plat of condominium may be recorded or be effective unless first approved by the City.

2\. Nothing contained in this Chapter shall exclude or exempt any condominium project from conforming to the requirements of the Condominium Property Act, 765 Illinois Compiled Statutes 605/1 et seq., as amended from time to time. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)

### 5-11-4: PREREQUISITE TO SUBMISSION TO STATE LAW

No real estate may be submitted to the Condominium Property Act, whether as new construction or as a condominium conversion, until compliance is had with all requirements of that Act, as amended from time to time, and with the requirements of this Chapter as applicable, as amended from time to time, and with all other applicable City Code provisions. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)

### 5-11-5: CITY CONDOMINIUM APPROVAL PROCESS

1\. Developer's Report Of Proposed Improvements And Alterations: If a developer decides to proceed with a condominium conversion, they shall submit an application provided by the City for a condominium conversion along with the application fee as provided in Section 5-11-7 of this Chapter and shall tender a written report ("developer's report") describing:

1.1. The developer's intent with respect to the improvements, replacements, and/or alterations to be made as part of the condominium conversion, including, but not limited to, the common elements, the limited common elements, the common systems, the condominium units, landscaping, signage, lighting, fire suppression systems, and any other information reasonably requested by the City. Unless the existing parking meets then current Code requirements, the developer shall submit a parking management plan setting forth a proposed strategy with respect to the provision of adequate parking for the condominium project.

1.2. A study shall be prepared and signed and sealed by a professional engineer, structural engineer, or architect stating as to the following improvements: 1) the approximate date of installation; 2) their current condition; 3) the date of any major repairs that have been made; 4) their expected remaining useful life; 5) an opinion as to whether such improvements require immediate major repair or replacement; and 6) the estimated cost of such replacement or repair at current market prices, understanding that said estimated cost of replacement or repair is an estimate only and is not intended to be a guarantee:

1.2.1. Roof;

1.2.2. Foundation;

1.2.3. External and supporting walls;

1.2.4. Mechanical systems;

1.2.5. Electrical systems;

1.2.6. Plumbing systems;

1.2.7. Appliances in the common elements and the limited common elements;

1.2.8. Cabinetry and countertops in the common elements and the limited common elements;

1.2.9. Windows and doors in the common elements and the limited common elements;

1.2.10. Floor coverings in the common elements and the limited common elements; and

1.2.11. Other structural elements as well as all other common facilities.

2\. Inspection/City Report: Upon submittal by the developer of an application for a condominium conversion, payment of the application fee, and a completed developer's report, the City shall inspect the condominium property and prepare a report ("city report") setting forth recommendations with respect to the following and such other matters pertaining to the condominium project and the property as are deemed necessary and appropriate in order to preserve the public health, safety and welfare: 1) the minimum requirements described in Section 5-11-6 of this Chapter, 2) landscaping, 3) signage, 4) lighting, 5) parking, 6) common elements/limited common elements, 7) common systems.

3\. Condominium Developer's Acknowledgement And Acceptance: Utilizing the developer's report, the City report, and such other information as may reasonably be requested of the developer by the City, specific conditions pertaining to the components described in this Chapter shall be incorporated in a document to be referenced as a condominium developer's acknowledgment and acceptance ("CDAA") to be executed by the developer in a form approved by the City Attorney, in order to obligate the developer to comply with the conditions set forth.

3.1. Compliance With City Code Requirements: The City may require compliance with all City Code requirements. However, in order to focus attention and resources on public health, welfare and safety issues of significance in an efficient and cost effective manner, the recommended CDAA may require less than strict and technical compliance with Code provisions, except for the minimum requirements set forth in Section 5-11-6 of this Chapter. Any variance from the minimum requirements, or any variance from a nonminimum requirement which the Chief Building Official has determined should be enforced in the condominium project, must be petitioned for by the developer to the City's Building Review Board as provided in this Title. The City Council may also direct that other aspects of the condominium project which do not comply with the Code be reviewed by the City's Building Review Board.

3.2. Provision Of Declaration And Other Condominium Documents: The developer of new condominium construction or a condominium conversion, shall provide the City with the notice of intent to submit the property or add on condominium property to the Condominium Property Act, the most up to date draft of the condominium declaration, all documents required by that Act, and any property report or other information provided to prospective condominium unit purchasers as soon as they become available.

3.3. Submission Of Condominium Plat Of Survey And Address Map: The developer of new condominium construction or a condominium conversion shall submit to the City an up to date address map and a proposed condom inium plat of survey. The condominium plat of survey shall be prepared and submitted in accord with the provisions of the Condominium Property Act, the Plat Act\* and this Chapter, including the specifications set forth in the "Naperville condominium plat of survey checklist" on file with the City. The developer shall cooperate with the City in promptly making revisions to the condominium plat of survey and address map as requested by the TED Business Group.

3.4. Ordinance Approving A Condominium Plat Of Survey: Upon finalization of the new condominium construction or condominium conversion plat and execution of a CDAA by the developer, an ordinance approving a condominium plat, specifically incorporating the CDAA as a condition of such approval, shall be submitted to the City Council for its consideration. The City Council may approve such plat and authorize and direct the Mayor and City Clerk to sign and attest such plat for and in the name of the City, and the City Clerk shall attach the corporate seal thereto. If the City Council determines that the condominium project is not in compliance with the requirements of the Condominium Property Act or of this Chapter, or that the provisions of the CDAA do not sufficiently address the public health, safety or welfare, the City Council may elect not to approve the ordinance approving the condominium plat of survey, or may alternatively require such other conditions to approval as may be deemed necessary and appropriate to protect the public interest. The ordinance may provide a period of time after which approval of the condominium plat shall not longer be effective. If the condominium plat of survey is not properly recorded with the declaration by the developer within one hundred eighty

(180) days from approval of the ordinance approving a condominium plat of survey, it shall no longer be effective or entitled to be recorded.

3.5. Recording Of Condominium Plat Of Survey: Subsequent to passage of an ordinance by the City Council approving the condominium plat of survey for new condominium construction or condominium conversion, the plant shall be signed and attested by the Mayor and City Clerk. The developer shall then record the condominium plat of survey and the declaration with the county recorder in the county where the property is located and shall submit recorded copies of each to the City Clerk within seven (7) days of receipt of the recorded documents from the recorder. The City Clerk shall record the ordinance approving a condominium plat of survey, along with the exhibits to the ordinance, including a copy of the plat which has been signed by the Mayor and attested by the clerk with the county recorder in the county where the property is located. No amended plat of condominium may be recorded or be effective unless first approved by the City.

### 5-11-6: MINIMUM CONDOMINIUM CONVERSION REQUIREMENTS

The following minimum requirements apply to all condominium conversion projects unless a variance is approved by the City Council, subsequent to review by the City's Building Review Board, which variance is incorporated into the ordinance approving the condominium plat of survey. These minimum requirements are in addition to other requirements contained in the CDAA.

1\. Fire Sprinkler Requirements: Any building which is being converted into residential condominiums shall be protected throughout by the developer with an automatic sprinkler system in

\* 765 ILCS 205/0.01 et seq. compliance with Section 903.3 of the International Building Code, pursuant to Section 5-1 A-1 of this Title, as amended from time to time. If a variance is requested to this requirement, one factor to consider shall be the economic hardship imposed by this requirement, in light of excessive cost per square foot. This could be due to availability of water supply, the complexity of the building's design, or lack of reasonable installation costs versus the average in the region; any or all which could detrimentally affect the viability of the project to convert the building from apartments to condos.

2\. Fire Alarms: An approved manual, automatic, or manual and automatic fire alarm system shall be installed in accordance with the provisions of this Title, NFPA 72, and the then current edition of the Naperville Fire Department "Regulations For The Installation, Maintenance And Testing Of Fire Alarm Systems" .

3\. Carbon Monoxide Detectors: Carbon monoxide detectors shall be installed and maintained in compliance with the laws of the State of Illinois in all residential and commercial condominiums.

4\. Accessibility: Unless determined to be technically infeasible pursuant to the reasonable judgment of the City's Chief Building Official, at least one accessible route within the property shall be provided from public transportation stops, accessible parking, accessible passenger loading zones, public streets or sidewalks and accessible facilities on the property such as a clubhouse or swimming pool. Provisions for accessibility shall be incorporated and made part of the CDAA.

5\. Common Elements And Common Systems: The following minimum requirements shall apply to "common elements" and "common systems" as defined herein:

5.1. Heating, cooling, hot water, roof covering, windows, patio doors and balconies, plumbing or electrical systems shall be replaced or repaired in accordance with the CDAA.

5.2. Items in the common elements, including, but not limited to, cabinets, countertops, refrigerators, dishwashers, floor coverings, lighting fixtures and interior plumbing fixtures must be replaced or repaired in accordance with the CDAA.

5.3. Battery backup electric exit signs shall be installed pursuant to the requirements of this Title.

5.4. Emergency lighting shall be installed pursuant to the requirements of this Title.

5.5. Self-closing devices shall be installed on all building and unit entry doors.

5.6. A Knox box on the exterior of buildings with keys for utility, meter rooms and furnace rooms shall be installed.

```text
         5.7        Landscaping shall be in accord with applicable provisions of this Code. Landscaping
```

requirements may be incorporated into the CDAA.

5.8. All public walks of the common elements are required to be in good order, stead and repair.

5.9. All laundry room appliances shall be replaced or repaired in accordance with the CDAA.

5.10. Exit corridors and egress elements shall be protected pursuant to the provisions of the International Building Code and this Title.

5.11. All refuse disposal shall be screened on four (4) sides by a solid commercial grade wood fence, wall, or equivalent material to a height of six (6) feet. The enclosure shall be used strictly for the confinement of refuse and shall not be used for the outside storage of any other materials or equipment. Doors must be hung no lower than three (3) inches from the ground. The door side of said enclosure shall be situated such that, to the greatest extent possible, it does not face towards an abutting property, sidewalk, or street.

5.12. Unless the existing parking meets then current Code requirements, the developer shall submit a parking management plan setting forth a proposed strategy with respect to the provision of adequate parking for the condominium project.

5.13. All plumbing fixtures will be in good working order, with water shutoffs at all water sources. Valving shall be arranged so that water service to each unit can be shut off without shutting off any other unit's service.

6\. Warranties: Any agreement for the sale of a unit pursuant to this Chapter shall contain a one-year warranty, to the extent provided herein, that the unit, the common elements, and the limited common elements are in good condition, free from any latent or patent defects and that the developer shall repair or replace any defect in said unit or common elements, or limited common elements at the developer's sole cost promptly upon being notified in writing of such defect by the purchaser of such unit or the condominium association, as the case may be; provided, however, that such notice of defect must be delivered to the developer within one year from the date of conveyance of title thereto to the purchaser. Insofar as this provision applies to warranties of common elements and limited common elements, the warranties shall be applicable only to common systems. Insofar as this provision applies to warranties of individual condominium units, the warranties shall be applicable only to refrigerators, stoves, garbage disposals, dishwashers, and air handling elements such as furnaces and air conditioners (not including window units). (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)

### 5-11-7: CONDOMINIUM PLAT REVIEW AND INSPECTION FEES

The following fees shall be applicable to condominium conversions. A copy of the applicable fee schedule for such fees, as revised from time to time, shall be available upon request.

1\. An application fee shall be paid by the developer at the time the condominium conversion application is filed, in order for the City to be reimbursed for its costs of evaluating the condominium conversion project proposal, including work performed by City inspectors, project managers, and clerical staff, including preparation of the City report.

2\. If the developer decides to proceed with the condominium project after receipt of the City report, the applicant will be responsible to pay the City a condominium processing fee for costs associated with processing the application not covered by the application fee, including, but not limited to, plan and plat reviews, inspection services and generation of necessary reports pertaining to the condominium project. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)

### 5-11-8: PROTECTION OFTENANTS

1\. In addition to the notice requirements set forth in Section 605/30 of the Condominium Property Act, the following shall also apply. Nothing herein shall be construed or interpreted to reduce or lessen the protections to tenants afforded by the Act.

1.1. Not less than ninety (90) days prior to offering for sale any condominium unit or recording the declaration pursuant to the Condominium Property Act, whichever occurs first, the developer shall give written notice of intent to each tenant of the building, if any, of the intent to submit the property to that Act. The notice of intent must set forth the rights of tenants under the Condominium Property Act and this Chapter and must include a copy of this Chapter in legible 12-point type. Such notice shall be hand delivered or sent by certified mail and regular mail.

1.2. Failure of the developer to give notice as required above is a defense to an action for possession, unless a tenant knowingly avoids receiving or delivery of such notice of intent.

2\. No tenant may be required to vacate their unit upon less than one hundred twenty (120) days' notice, except by reason of nonpayment of rent, conduct that disturbs other tenants' peaceful enjoyment of the premises, or other substantial violations of the terms of the rental agreement. The terms of tenancy may not be altered during that period.

3\. Nothing in this Section permits termination of a lease in violation of its terms.

4\. No lease or other agreement for the rental of any part of the property shall contain any provision which authorizes the lessor to terminate the lease on lesser notice or in a manner less favorable than provided for herein.

5\. Except as otherwise provided herein, tenants' right to purchase shall be as set forth in Section 30 of the Condominium Property Act.

6\. Tenants shall have the rights of first refusal afforded by the Act.

7\. Notwithstanding lease provisions to the contrary, a tenant's premises shall not be shown to prospective purchasers without the tenant's permission until the expiration of the tenant's right to purchase or ninety (90) days prior to the end of the tenant's lease, whichever is later. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 5-11-9: CONDOMINIUM DECLARATION PROVISIONS

1\. The declaration shall include a provision specifically prohibiting waiver of all or part of the reserve requirements of Section 605/9 of the Condominium Property Act. The declaration shall further provide that any subsequent amendments to the declaration affecting that provision shall require approval by the City.

2\. The declaration shall provide that a recorded copy of any amendment to the declaration shall be submitted to the City as soon as practicable.

3\. The declaration shall include a provision that states that any amendment to the condominium plat must be approved by the City of Naperville. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)

### 5-11-10: EASEMENTS AND DEDICATIONS

1\. The developer or board of managers shall grant or dedicate to the City, at no cost, those easements and rights-of-way which the City indicates that it reasonably needs in order to provide, or to continue to provide or facilitate, utility or other City services to the condominium project or other property. Such easements and rights-of-way shall not materially impact the use of the property. The City or its designees shall, upon completion of any work authorized pursuant to this Section, restore the surface of the easement premises to the same or better surface condition than that which existed prior to the beginning of any work.

2\. The developer shall ensure that the condominium instruments include a provision requiring that the owners of the condominium property adequately maintain and repair any storm water management facilities located on the property. If requested by the City, the developer or board of managers shall grant the City an easement on the property so that in the event that storm water management on the property is not adequately maintained or repaired in the future, the City shall have the right, but not the obligation, to access the property for purposes of maintaining or repairing such storm water management facilities, the cost of which may be assessed against the property owners or may be filed as a lien against the property.

3\. All grants of easement or dedication required from the developer pursuant to this Section shall be referenced in the CDAA, and any plats of easement or dedication shall be prepared at the developer's cost, in a form approved by the City Attorney, reviewed by the City, and shall be attached as an exhibit to CDAA unless otherwise provided for in the CDAA. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)

### 5-11-11: ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES

1\. Complaints: The Chief Building Official is hereby authorized and directed to receive complaints and conduct such investigations of violations of this Chapter as they deem necessary.

2\. Violations: In the event that the Chief Building Official determines that a violation of this Chapter exists, and the same is not remedied, then they may seek assistance from the City Attorney to apply to any court of competent jurisdiction to enjoin any violation of this Chapter, to seek fines or penalties, or to take such other and further action as they, in their discretion, may deem necessary and appropriate.

3\. Penalties: In addition to any other remedies sought by the City, any person or entity which violates, disobeys, omits, neglects, or refuses to comply with or who resists enforcement of any of the provisions of this Chapter shall, upon being found guilty of an ordinance violation, be fined within a range of one hundred dollars ($100.00) to five hundred dollars ($500.00) per day.

4\. Rights, Obligations And Remedies: The rights, obligations and remedies set forth herein shall be cumulative and in addition to any other rights available at law or in equity. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 5-11-12: REMOVAL FROM THE ACT

Whenever condominium property within the City is removed from the provisions of the Condominium Property Act pursuant to the provisions of Section 605/16 of that Act, and such removal is recorded with the appropriate county recorder, the City Clerk shall be notified by the then acting president of the board of managers or association of the condominium project of the removal and shall be provided with a copy of the instrument by which such removal was effectuated. (Ord. No. 08-037, 2-19-2008)
