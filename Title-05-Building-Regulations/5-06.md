# 5-6 FLOOD HAZARD AREAS

SECTION:\
5-6-1: Flood Plain Regulations

### 5-6-1: FLOOD PLAIN REGULATIONS

The flood plain regulations for development in special flood hazard areas in the DuPage County portion of the City as contained in the DuPage Countywide Stormwater and Floodplain Ordinance, effective May 14, 2019, as amended from time to time, are hereby adopted by reference. One (1) copy of said regulations, effective August 1, 2019, shall be kept on file for public inspection in the office of the City Engineer. (Ord. 99-103, 6-15-1999; Ord. No. 12-094, § 3, 7-17-2012; Ord. No. 13-059, § 3, 6-18-2013; Ord. No. 19-083, § 2, 6-18-2019)

## CHAPTER 6A. FLOOD HAZARD AREAS

SECTION:\
5-6A-1: Flood Plain Regulations

### 5-6A-1: FLOOD PLAIN REGULATIONS

The flood plain regulations for development in special flood hazard areas in the Will County portion of the City of Naperville, Illinois, as contained in the Will County Stormwater Management Ordinance, effective November 28, 2018, as amended from time to time, are hereby adopted by reference. One (1) copy of said regulations, effective February 15, 2019, shall be kept on file for public inspection in the office of the City Engineer. (Ord. No. 95-111, 6-6-1995; Ord. No. 19-003, § 2, 1-15-2019)
