# 5-8 OFF STREET PARKING FACILITIES

### 5-8-1: BUILDING PERMITS

No building permit shall be issued under the provisions of this Title until the applicant has presented satisfactory evidence to the Chief Building Official that the parking facilities provided by this Chapter will be provided and maintained. Such evidence shall include, but is not limited to, detailed plans and specifications of the proposed off street parking facilities. Such plans and specifications shall demonstrate conformance to the requirements and minimum parking standards of this Chapter and of the zoning ordinance of the City[^7].

(Ord. 02-137, 6-4-2002)

### 5-8-2: OFF STREET PARKING STANDARDS

Detailed plans and specifications for all off street parking lots, other than for single-family residences, consisting of more than two (2) parking stalls or more than eight hundred fifty (850) square feet of pavement area, shall be submitted to the City Engineer for review and approval prior to the issuance of a building permit. Said plans shall demonstrate conformance with the following ordinances and specifications:

1\. Naperville zoning ordinance 80-5, as amended (Title 6 of this Code);

2\. Naperville "Design Manual For Public Improvements", as amended from time to time;

3\. Naperville subdivision control ordinance 76-34, as amended (Title 7 of this Code).

(Ord. 02-137, 6-4-2002)

### 5-8-3: PLANS AND SPECIFICATIONS

At least eight (8) sets of detailed plans and specifications for the proposed off street parking facility shall be submitted with applicant's building permit application which shall include:

(Ord. 02-137, 6-4-2002)

1\. Traffic data showing the nature, extent and duration of parking use to be generated for the proposed facility;

2\. Existing topography of the proposed site and of adjacent areas within fifty (50) feet of the proposed facility;

3\. Existing and proposed utilities and utility easements, including, but not limited to, sanitary sewers, water lines, storm sewers, drainage swales, manholes, service lines, electric and telephone facilities, gas mains, traffic signals and any other existing underground or lighting facility;

4\. Existing and proposed buildings or structures within fifty (50) feet of the proposed facility;

5\. Existing and proposed streets and driveways on or adjacent to the proposed site;

6\. Proposed storm water management design plans;

7\. Existing and proposed property lines;

8\. North arrow and scale;

9\. Certification of a registered professional engineer responsible for preparation of the detailed plans and specifications for the proposed facility.

(Ord. 83-124, 12-19-1983)

### 5-8-4: AS BUILT DRAWINGS

As built drawings of the facility shall be submitted to the City Engineer prior to issuance of an occupancy permit for the facility. The City Engineer shall, within five (5) working days of submission of said drawings, approve or disapprove in writing the issuance of an occupancy permit for the facility.

(Ord. 83-124, 12-19-1983)

### 5-8-5: MAINTENANCE OF FACILITIES

Off street parking facilities required by this Chapter or by the zoning ordinance of The City shall be maintained so long as the structure or use for which they are provided exists, unless an equivalent number of such spaces is provided and thereafter maintained in conformance with the requirements of this Chapter. In no event shall parking facilities which are provided or maintained for a structure or use be considered as providing any of the required space for any other structure or use.

(Ord. 83-124, 12-19-1983)

### 5-8-6: CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY

Occupancy of any off street parking facilities required by this Chapter without a certificate of occupancy therefor is unlawful. The Chief Building Official shall issue a certificate of occupancy for any such structure within seven (7) working days of a submission by the applicant of as built drawings of the facility if said drawings demonstrate compliance with this Chapter.

(Ord. 02-137, 6-4-2002)

### 5-8-7: OTHER PROVISIONS IN EFFECT

Nothing in this Chapter shall be interpreted to repeal any off street parking regulation imposed pursuant to the zoning ordinance of the City.

(Ord. 02-137, 6-4-2002)

### 5-8-8: PENALTY

Any person violating any of the provisions of this Chapter shall be fined not less than twenty dollars (\$20.00) nor more than five hundred dollars (\$500.00) for each offense. A separate offense shall be deemed committed on each day during or on which a violation occurs or continues.

(Ord. 83-124, 12-19-1983)

[^7]: See Title 6, Chapter 9 of this Code.
