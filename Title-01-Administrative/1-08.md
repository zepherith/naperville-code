# 1-8 MUNICIPAL DEPARTMENTS

## ARTICLE A. POLICE DEPARTMENT

### 1-8A-1: APPOINTMENTS AND DISCHARGES

1\. All appointments to and promotions within the Police Department, except that of the Chief and the management ranks of Deputy Chief of Police and Police Commander, shall be made by the Board of Fire and Police Commissioners as approved by statute, and no police officer, except the Chief of Police and the management ranks of Deputy Chief of Police and Police Commander, shall be discharged from the Department except after a hearing before the Board of Fire and Police Commissioners as provided by law.[^2]

2\. The Chief of Police shall have the authority to recruit and appoint individuals to the positions of Deputy Chief of Police and Commander. The City Manager shall have final approval of all appointments. Potential appointees may be appointed from any rank of sworn office or from outside of the organization. A Deputy Chief of Police and a Commander shall serve at the pleasure of the Chief of Police. The Police Chief may suspend a Deputy Chief of Police and a Commander for up to three (3) days or recommend a longer suspension, a demotion or dismissal to the City Manager. Without review of the Board of Fire and Police Commissioners, the City Manager may act on disciplinary matters. The positions of Deputy Chief of Police and Commander are specifically exempt from the provisions of 65 Illinois Compiled Statutes 5/10-2.1-1 et seq. If a Deputy Chief of Police or Commander is appointed from the ranks of the Naperville Police Department, the Chief of Police may, at their discretion, at any time recommend demotion of the officer to the position held prior to the appointment.

3\. All applicants for positions as sworn personnel within the Police Department shall be citizens of the United States of America.

(Ord. No. 03-36, 2-4-2003; Ord. No. 07-154, 7-17-2007; Ord. No. 08-229, 12-16-2008; Ord. No. 10-023, § 1, 3-15-2010; Ord. No. 10-146, § 1, 12-7-2010; Ord. No. 11-089, § 1, 6-21-2011; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8A-2: CHIEF OF POLICE; DUTIES

1\. Duties Of The Chief Of Police: The Chief of Police shall be the keeper of the City jail[^3], and shall have custody of all persons incarcerated therein. They shall keep records and make reports concerning the activities of the Department as may be required by statute or by the City Council. The Chief shall be responsible for the performance of the Police Department, and all persons who are members of the Department shall serve subject to their orders. The Chief shall also be the custodian of all lost and abandoned or stolen property in the City.

2\. Authority To Develop Standard Operating Procedures And Rules And Regulations: The Chief of Police shall develop standard operating procedures and establish rules and regulations for the conduct and guidance of Police Department personnel as they deem advisable; such procedures and rules, when approved by the City Manager, shall be binding on such personnel.

(1960 Code, §§ 3.004, 3.011; amd. 1984 Code; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8A-3: POLICE OFFICERS' POWERS, DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

1\. General: It shall be the duty of the members of the Police Department to see to the enforcement of all of the ordinances of the City and all statutes applicable herein; and to preserve order and prevent infractions of the law, and arrest violators thereof.

2\. Service Process: No member of the Police Department, except the Chief, shall serve any process except on command of their superior.

3\. Conduct Of Members: It shall be the duty of every member of the Police Department to conduct themselves in a proper and law abiding manner at all times, and to avoid the use of unnecessary force. Each member of the Department shall obey the order and directions of their superior.

4\. Witness Fees: Any member of the Police Department shall appear as a witness whenever this is necessary in a prosecution for a violation of an ordinance or of any State or federal law. No such member shall retain any witness fee for service as a witness in any action or suit to which the City is a party; and fees paid for such services shall be turned over to the Chief of Police, who shall deposit the same with the City Treasurer.

(1960 Code, §§ 3.005—3.008; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8A-4: DEPARTMENT MEMBERS NOT CITY OFFICERS

Members of the Police Department are not City officers. The provision of 65 Illinois Compiled Statutes 5/10-2.1-4, stating that any full time member of a regular Police or Fire Department is a City officer, shall not be applicable within this City. All other provisions of 65 Illinois Compiled Statutes 5/10-2.1 shall be in full force and effect within this City.

(Ord. No. 76-9, 1-19-1976)

### 1-8A-5: LEADS COMMUNICATIONS

1\. The Chief of Police shall control and manage all LEADS related equipment and communications relative thereto including all LEADS terminals, printers and related equipment and all radio and telecommunications equipment.

2\. No person shall have access, operate or use any LEADS terminals, printers, telecommunications equipment or related equipment without the approval of the Chief of Police.

3\. No person shall obtain, disseminate or use any LEADS generated data or information without the prior approval of the Chief of Police.

4\. Violations of Subsection 1-8A-5.2 or 1-8A-5.3 of this Section shall be cause for suspension or discharge of any such violator from service with the City.

5\. The selection of software relating to LEADS shall be subject to approval of the Chief of Police.

6\. The Chief of Police shall have authority to inspect and audit the City's complete system as it relates to LEADS and to grant permission to LEADS to inspect and audit the system.

7\. Nothing contained herein shall be deemed to usurp the authority vested by law in the corporate authorities of the City.

(Ord. No. 84-124, 9-4-1984)

## ARTICLE B. FIRE DEPARTMENT

### 1-8B-1: FIRE DEPARTMENT CREATED, MEMBERSHIP

1\. Regular Fire Department: There is hereby created the Fire Department, a principal department of the City. The Director of the Fire Department shall be the Fire Chief. In addition, there may be such full time positions as may be authorized by the City Council and approved by the City Manager.

1.1. The Fire Chief shall be appointed by, may be removed by, and shall be responsible to the City Manager.

1.2. Reserved.

1.3. Except for the Fire Chief, Deputy Chiefs, and Battalion Chief/Bureau Chiefs/Division Chiefs, the Board of Fire and Police Commissioners shall be responsible for appointment of and personnel actions concerning full time, sworn personnel, as provided by statute[^4].

1.4. All applicants for positions as sworn personnel within the Fire Department shall be citizens of the United States of America.

2\. Department Members Not City Officers: Members of the Fire Department are not City officers.

2.1. The provision of 65 Illinois Compiled Statutes 5/10-2.1-4, specifying that any full time members of a regular police or Fire Department is a City officer, shall not apply within this City.

2.2. All other provisions of 65 Illinois Compiled Statutes 5/10-2.1 shall be in full force and effect within this City except as otherwise provided by this Code.

3\. Appointment of Deputy Chief and Battalion Chief/Bureau Chief/Division Chief: The Fire Chief shall have authority to recruit and recommend individuals for appointment to Deputy Fire Chief and Battalion Chief/Bureau Chief/Division Chief. The City Manager shall have final approval of all such appointments.

3.1. Successful candidates for Deputy Chief and Battalion Chief/Bureau Chief and Division Chief may be selected from within or outside of the ranks of the Fire Department.

3.2. The Deputy Chief and Battalion Chief/Bureau Chief/Division Chief shall serve at the pleasure of the Fire Chief.

3.3. The Fire Chief may suspend a Deputy Chief or a Battalion Chief/Bureau Chief/Division Chief for up to three (3) days. Any longer suspension, demotion or dismissal shall be approved by the City Manager without review by the Board of Fire and Police Commissioners.

(Ord. No. 98-51, 4-21-1998; Ord. No. 98-52, 4-21-1998; Ord. No. 03-35, 2-4-2003; Ord. No. 05-094, 6-7-2005; amd. Ord. No. 07-135, 6-19-2007; Ord. No. 08-229, 12-16-2008; Ord. No. 09-074, § 1, 6-16-2009; Ord. No. 10-024, § 1, 3-15-2010; Ord. No. 10-119, § 1, 9-21-2010; Ord. No. 10-153, § 1, 12-7-2010; Ord. No. 11-089, § 2, 6-21-2011)

### 1-8B-2: FIRE CHIEF, POWERS AND AUTHORITY

1\. Duties Of Fire Chief: The Fire Chief shall be responsible for the care of the apparatus belonging thereto and for the conduct of the members of the Fire Department. They shall keep or cause to be kept such records and make such reports showing the activities of their department as may be required by the City Manager and City Council.

2\. Authority To Develop Standard Operating Procedures: The Fire Chief shall develop standard operating procedures for the conduct and guidance of the Fire Department personnel as they shall deem advisable; and such standard operating procedures, when approved by the City Manager, shall be binding on such members.

(Ord. No. 72-10, 3-13-1972; amd. 1984 Code; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8B-3: CHAIN OF COMMAND

The Fire Chief, and in their absence a Deputy Fire Chief, and in the absence of a Deputy Fire Chief, a Chief Fire Officer from the Department chosen by the Fire Chief shall have full responsibility and authority for the operation of the Department as specified in this Article. All departmental personnel shall obey the orders of their superior officers while on duty.

(Ord. No. 98-51, 4-21-1998; amd. Ord. No. 05-094, 6-7-2005; Ord. No. 07-135, 6-19-2007; Ord. No. 10-153, § 2, 12-7-2010; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8B-4: GENERAL POWERS AND DUTIES

1\. The Fire Department shall be responsible for preventing, extinguishing and investigating the causes of fire occurring in the City, enforcement of the provisions of this Code and all City ordinances relating to fire hazards, reporting possible violations of this Code or of any City ordinance in connection with any fire to the City Attorney. The Fire Department shall have such other duties and responsibilities as may be prescribed by law, and as may be assigned to it by the City Manager, including, but not limited to, hazardous materials response, technical rescue, underwater recovery and public education. These specified powers and duties shall apply throughout all or part of any fire district served by the Fire Department pursuant to agreement.

2\. The Fire Department shall be responsible for maintaining and providing ambulance service within the City and within such additional areas which are to be served under contract. The fees shall be increased annually, effective January 1 of each year, beginning in 2021, without further City Council action, pursuant to the annual report submitted for the previous year to the Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services (HFS) and/or the federal Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) reflecting the actual cost incurred for transport of residents and nonresidents. Ambulance user fees shall be assessed as follows:

2.1. Each resident who is treated on scene and requiring Advanced or Basic Life Support Services or treated on scene and transported by ambulance shall be charged a fee that has been established by the Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services and listed in the cost report as outlined in Subsection 2, above. A resident constitutes any person whose permanent address is within the City's corporate limits or within the Naperville Fire Protection District.

2.2. Reserved.

2.3. Reserved.

2.4. Each nonresident who is treated on scene and requiring Advanced or Basic Life Support Services or treated on scene and is transported by ambulance shall be charged a fee that has been established by the Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services (HFS) and listed in the cost report as outlined in Section 2, above. A nonresident constitutes any person whose permanent address of residence is neither within the City's corporate limits nor within the Naperville Fire Protection District.

2.5. Reserved.

2.6. Reserved.

2.7. Reserved.

2.8. Reserved.

2.9. All patients transported by ambulance shall be charged a mileage fee equal to the distance traveled to transport the patient to the hospital at fifteen dollars (\$15.00) per mile.

2.10. When ambulance or emergency services are rendered by the City of Naperville to residents, such persons shall not be billed for the remaining charges which may exist once final payment has been received from all insurance providers of the resident.

2.11. When ambulance or emergency services are rendered by the City of Naperville to non-residents, such persons shall be billed for the remaining charges which may exist once final payment has been received from all insurance providers of the non-resident. Unpaid balances shall be assigned to a debt-collection agency of the City's choice.

3\. The Fire Chief, Deputy Fire Chiefs, Battalion/Bureau/Division Fire Chiefs, the captains and lieutenants of the Fire Department shall have the power and authority to cause to be arrested any person, at or near the scene of an emergency incident, for any interference with the Fire Department in the performance of its duties or for the commission of any crime or violation of any ordinance in connection with the emergency incident.

4\. The Naperville Fire Department shall include a fee for the purpose of recovering the cost of fire services incurred in relation to responses for vehicle accidents and vehicle fire incidents where operational vehicles responded and performed services. Fees shall be assessed as follows:

4.1. The response fee for responding to a motor vehicle accident shall be five hundred fifty dollars (\$550.00).

4.2. The response fee for responding to a vehicle fire shall be six hundred five dollars (\$605.00).

4.3. When fire response services are rendered by the City of Naperville to residents, such persons shall not be billed for the remaining charges which may exist once final payment has been received from all insurance providers of the resident.

4.4. When fire response services are rendered by the City of Naperville to non-residents, such persons shall be billed for the remaining charges which may exist once final payment has been received from all insurance providers of the non-resident. Unpaid balances shall be assigned to a debt-collection agency of the City's choice.

4.4. Each resident or non-resident provided mobile integrated health care services on-site by the Fire Department emergency medical services personnel shall be billed for such services. Mobile integrated health care services shall be defined as medically necessary health services provided on-site by emergency medical services personnel including those services listed in 235 ILCS 5/356z.71(a) and minor medical procedures as approved by the EMS Medical Director. Charges for services shall be based on level or service and time and based upon rates set by the Illinois Department of Healthcare and Family Services.

4.5. Each assisted living facility or nursing home facility requesting more than six (6) calls for lift assist service per year from the Fire Department shall be charged four hundred dollars (\$400.00) per lift assist service starting after the sixth (6th) call that year. Lift assist service shall mean a response from the Fire Department to an assisted living or nursing facility in order to lift a patient or other individual from the individual's current position to a desired position. Lift assist service does not include lifting a patient during a response to a request for transportation to a health care facility such as a hospital or emergency room.

(Ord. No. 98-51, 4-21-1998; Amd. Ord. 05-094, 6-7-2005; Ord. 07-135, 6-19-2007; Ord. No. 08-065, 4-1-2008; Ord. No. 09-108, § 1, 9-15-2009; Ord. No. 10-153, § 3, 12-7-2010; Ord. No. 16-173, § 1, 10-18-2016; Ord. No. 20-128, § 1, 12-1-2020; Ord. No. 26-048, § 2, 5-19-2026)

### 1-8B-5: CONDUCT, DUTIES AT EMERGENCY INCIDENTS

The Fire Chief, or in their absence the officer in command at any emergency incident, shall have the power to cause the removal of any property whenever it shall become necessary for the preservation of life and property or to protect the adjoining property; and may direct the members of the Fire Department to cut down and remove any building, structure, or fence for the purpose of checking the progress of any emergency incident.

1\. The Fire Chief, or any other officer in command at any emergency incident, may prescribe limits in the vicinity of any emergency incident within which no persons, except firefighters, police officers, and those admitted by any officer of the Fire Department, shall be permitted to go, and the Department of Police shall, upon the request of the Fire Chief or other officer in command of the Fire Department at any emergency incident, enforce the provisions of this Section.

2\. The Fire Chief and other officers of the Fire Department in command at any emergency incident shall have the right to give orders to any person present at any emergency incident concerning the abatement of said emergency and the removal and protection of property and life, and in case any such person shall refuse to obey such orders, such person shall be subject to arrest, as provided by law.

3\. The Fire Chief or their designated subordinate shall investigate the cause and origin of all fires and explosions resulting from accident, unknown or suspicious circumstances, arson or attempted arson. The Fire Chief shall investigate all incidents involving fire related deaths or injuries, or the discovery of explosives or incendiary devices. When an investigation involves an entry onto private property, the investigator shall attempt to secure the permission of the owner or occupant of the premises. If permission is refused, or if the owner or occupant is unavailable, the investigator shall, with the assistance of the City Attorney, apply to the appropriate court for the issuance of a search warrant.

4\. If an investigator discovers or suspects a criminal act or violation of any City ordinance, they shall report same to the City Attorney.

(Ord. No. 92-205, 11-3-1992; Ord. No. 98-51, 4-21-1998; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8B-6: AID OUTSIDE CORPORATE LIMITS

Members of the Fire Department are authorized to travel and act outside the corporate limits of the City for the purpose of rendering aid to other Fire Departments or municipalities in extinguishing fires or rendering aid in case of accidents in accordance with the provisions of this Section:

1\. Where the Fire Department is acting pursuant to the terms and conditions of a contract or mutual aid agreement to render service to property outside the corporate limits; or

2\. Where either the Fire Chief or the City Manager has ordered such action; or

3\. Where the Fire Department is acting in accordance with provisions of a mutual aid box alarm system agreement, approved by resolution 97-5, dated February 18, 1997.

(Ord. No. 98-51, 4-21-1998)

### 1-8B-7: MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS AND PROHIBITIONS

1\. It shall be unlawful for any person to drive any vehicle, railroad engine or railroad car, over any unprotected fire hose laid at a fire, where the hose is in use by order of the Fire Chief or other fire officer in command, except by permission of the Fire Chief or fire officer in command. It shall be unlawful for any person not a member of the Fire Department to impersonate a firefighter or officer of the Fire Department at any emergency incident, or going to or returning from an emergency incident, by wearing a uniform, cap, insignia or badge, or in any other way attempting to create the impression that they are member or official of the Fire Department.

2\. It shall be unlawful for any person to interfere with a firefighter in the performance of their duty, or to refuse or neglect to furnish any member of the Fire Department help or information pertaining to the cause or origin of any fire, or to hinder any fire investigation in any manner.

3\. It shall be unlawful for any person in any manner to wilfully destroy, injure or deface any engine or other fire apparatus belonging to the City.

4\. It shall be unlawful for any person to take away any property in the possession of the Fire Department saved from any emergency incident, until proof of ownership shall be made to the satisfaction of the Fire Chief or said officer, or the officer in command at the emergency incident, and until authorized to remove said property by the Fire Chief or said officer.

5\. It shall be unlawful for any person to negligently, maliciously or wilfully give any false alarm to any police or fire station through the use of any emergency alarm box; and further, that it shall be unlawful for any person to open any emergency alarm box for a purpose other than reporting an emergency matter to the Fire or Police Department, nor break, cut, injure, deface, damage or in any manner meddle or interfere with any emergency alarm box, or any municipal electric wires, poles, conduits or apparatus whatever.

6\. It shall be unlawful for any person, other than the members of the Fire Department for the uses and purposes of such Department, and those specifically authorized by the Department of Public Works, to open any public fire hydrant or attempt to draw water from the same; and further, that it shall be unlawful for any person to wilfully or carelessly break, injure or interfere with any of the public fire hydrants in the City.

7\. It shall be unlawful for any person in any manner to obstruct the use of any public fire hydrant, or place any material or vehicle in front thereof, or within fifteen (15) feet thereof at any curb; said distance to be measured from that point on the curb perpendicular to the exact location of the fire hydrant. Any and all material, or any vehicle found to be such an obstruction or within said fifteen (15) feet, may be immediately removed by any member of the Fire Department, and at the risk, cost, and expense of the owner or claimant thereof.

(Ord. No. 72-10, 3-13-1972; Ord. No. 92-205, 11-3-1992; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

## ARTICLE C. LEGAL DEPARTMENT

### 1-8C-1: CREATION OF OFFICE

The Department Head of the Legal Department shall be designated as the City Attorney and shall be hired by the City Manager. Additional personnel may be hired from time to time after approval by the City Manager, to give clerical, docket, and/or other assistance to the City Attorney, as the City Attorney may deem necessary and as provided for by duly adopted budgets of the City.

(Ord. No. 87-136, 9-8-1987)

### 1-8C-2: POWERS AND DUTIES

The City Attorney may:

1\. Suits And Actions: Prosecute or defend any and all suits or actions at law or equity to which the City may be a party, or in which it may be interested, or which may be brought against, or by, any officer of the City, or in the capacity of such person as an officer of the City.

2\. Judgments: See to the full enforcement of all judgments or decrees rendered or entered in favor of the City, and of all similar interlocutory orders.

3\. Advice: Render advice on all legal questions affecting the City, whenever requested to so do by any City official. Upon request by the City Manager, the City Attorney shall reduce any such opinion to writing.

4\. Legal Opinions: Prepare a formal opinion upon any legal matter or question submitted by the Mayor, the City Council or the head of any department upon the approval of the City Manager.

5\. Attend Council And Other Meetings: Attend Council meetings and such other City-related meetings as directed by the City Manager.

6\. Special Assessments: Direct legal services in connection with all special assessment proceedings, special service district proceedings, condemnation proceedings and all other such related litigation.

7\. Ordinances And Documents: Draft or supervise the phraseology of any contract, lease or other documents or instruments, to which the City may be a party; and upon request of the City Manager, draft ordinances or revise ordinances covering any subject within the power of the City.

(Ord. No. 87-136, 9-8-1987)

### 1-8C-3: REPORTS AND RECORDS

1\. The City Attorney shall make reports as follows:

1.1. Immediate Report Of Decision: Immediately report the outcome of any litigation in which the City has an interest to the City Manager and City Council.

1.2. Periodic Reports Of Pending Litigation: Make periodic reports, as requested, to the City Manager and City Council of all pending litigation in which the City has an interest and the status thereof.

2\. The City Attorney shall keep records as follows:

2.1. Suits: Keep a record of all suits in which the City has or had an interest, giving the names of the parties, the court that has or had jurisdiction, the nature of the action, the disposition of the case, or its status if pending, and the briefs of counsel.

2.2. Opinions And Titles: Keep a complete record of all written opinions by the City Attorney or the Corporation Counsel to the City, or any department or official thereof.

3\. The City Attorney shall deliver all records, documents, and property of every description in the City Attorney's possession, belonging to the office of the City Attorney or to the City, to the successor in office who shall give duplicate receipts therefor, one of which shall be filed with the City Clerk.

(Ord. No. 87-136, 9-8-1987)

### 1-8C-4: ADDITIONAL RESPONSIBILITIES

1\. The City Attorney shall consult with and coordinate the activities of the Legal Department with the Corporation Counsel. Both the City Attorney and the Corporation Counsel shall conduct their duties so as to avoid duplication of efforts and to minimize the legal expense of the City.

2\. The City Attorney, as a full-time City employee, shall work such hours as shall be necessary to perform the duties required by the City Manager and shall not conduct any private practice of law which would create a conflict of interest with their City employment, or which may be prohibited by the City Manager.

(Ord. No. 94-90, 6-7-1994; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

## ARTICLE D. EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY

### 1-8D-1: SHORT TITLE

This Article shall be known and may be cited and referred to as the *EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY CODE* of the City of Naperville, Illinois.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)

### 1-8D-2: POLICY AND PROCEDURES

1\. Because of the possibility of the occurrence of disasters of unprecedented size and destructiveness resulting from the explosion in this or in a neighboring municipality of atomic (devices) or other means from without, or by means of sabotage or other disloyal actions within, or from fire, flood, earthquake, tornado, or other natural or technological causes, and in order to ensure that this Municipality will be prepared to and will adequately deal with any such disasters, preserve the lives and the property of the people of this Municipality and protect the public peace, health and safety in the event of such a disaster, it is found and declared necessary to:

1.1. Create a Naperville Emergency Management Agency (EMA);

1.2. Confer upon the Mayor the extraordinary powers provided herein[^5];

1.3. Provide the rendering of mutual aid to other cities and political subdivisions and with respect to the carrying out of an emergency management program.

2\. Whenever the Mayor determines after an investigation that a dangerous situation or potentially dangerous situation exists which could cause death to individuals or serious injury to property or the health and welfare of the public, the Mayor may declare that a state of emergency exists. The extraordinary powers may not be exercised until a formal declaration shall have been issued by the Mayor, finding that such standards have been met, setting forth facts to substantiate such findings, describing the nature of the emergency and declaring that a state of emergency exists. This statement shall be filed with the City Clerk as soon as practical. A state of emergency shall expire not later than the adjournment of the first regular meeting of the corporate authorities after the state of emergency is declared. A subsequent state of emergency may be declared if necessary.

3\. It is further declared to be the purpose of this Article and the policy of this Municipality that all emergency management functions of this Municipality be coordinated to the maximum extent with the comparable functions of the federal and state governments, including their various departments and agencies, of other municipalities and localities and private agencies of every type, to the end that the most effective preparation and use may be made of the nation's manpower, resources, and facilities for dealing with any disaster that may occur.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)

### 1-8D-3: LIMITATIONS

Nothing in this Article shall be construed to:

1\. Interfere with the course or conduct of a labor dispute, except that actions otherwise authorized by this Article or other laws may be taken when necessary to mitigate imminent or existing danger to public health or safety;

2\. Interfere with dissemination of news or comment of public affairs; but any communications facility or organization (including, but not limited to, radio and television stations, wire services, and newspapers) may be required to transmit or print public service messages furnishing information or instructions in connection with a disaster;

3\. Affect the jurisdiction or responsibilities of police forces, firefighting forces, units of the armed forces of the United States, or of any personnel thereof, when on active duty; but State, local and interjurisdictional emergency operation plans shall place reliance upon forces available for performance of functions related to emergency management;

4\. Limit, modify or abridge the authority of the Mayor and the City Council to exercise any other powers vested in them under the Constitution, statutes, or common law of this State, independent of or in conjunction with any provisions of this Article or prohibit any contract or association pursuant to Article VII, Section 10 of the Illinois Constitution.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)

### 1-8D-5: ORGANIZATION AND APPOINTMENTS

1\. The City Manager is hereby authorized and directed to create a Naperville emergency management agency utilizing to the fullest extent the existing agencies within the City and shall be responsible for their emergency organization, administration and operations.

2\. The organization shall consist of the following:

2.1. A Naperville emergency management agency within the executive branch of the City government and under the general direction of the City Manager which shall be organized and staffed as from time to time determined by the City Manager within budgetary limits.

2.2. The employees, equipment and facilities of all City departments, boards, institutions and commissions, shall participate in the emergency services activity.

2.3. Volunteers whose offers of assistance have been accepted by the City whose functions shall include, but are not limited to, participation in traffic and crowd control, communications, first aid, severe weather tracking and spotting, search parties, public relations/education activities, training, training exercises with emergency and nonemergency assistance to other agencies and jurisdictions outside the City's corporate limits, hazardous materials response operations, Emergency Management Agency functions as may be prescribed by the governor of the State, and duties outside the City's corporate limits as may be required pursuant to any mutual aid agreement entered into by the City with any other political subdivision, municipality, taxing district or quasi-municipality, or as provided by the Illinois Emergency Management Agency Act, 20 Illinois Compiled Statutes 3305/1 et seq. (1994), as amended.

2.4. The City Manager shall appoint an Emergency Management Coordinator of the Naperville Emergency Management Agency. The Emergency Management Coordinator shall have such powers and duties as set forth herein, as provided pursuant to the Act for the Coordinator, and as from time to time designated by the City Manager.

2.5. The City Manager may designate such Deputy Emergency Management Coordinators as the City Manager deems appropriate to assume the emergency duties of Emergency Management Coordinator in the event of the Emergency Management Coordinator's absence or inability to act.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)

### 1-8D-6: DUTIES

1\. The Naperville Emergency Management Agency shall take an integral part in the development and revision of local and interjurisdictional disaster plans.

2\. In the development of local and interjurisdictional disaster plans, the Naperville Emergency Management Agency shall interrelate with business, labor, industry, taxing districts, other government agencies, civic and volunteer organizations and community leaders.

3\. The Naperville Emergency Management Agency shall:

3.1. Determine the requirements of the City for food, clothing and other necessities in the event of an emergency;

3.2. Promulgate standards and requirements for local and interjurisdictional disaster plans, in accordance with State and federal guidelines;

3.3. Periodically review local and interjurisdictional disaster plans and recommend revisions pursuant to State and federal rules;

3.4. Establish a register of persons with types of emergency management training and skills in mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery;

3.5. Establish a register of government and private response resources available for use in a disaster;

3.6. Develop an earthquake awareness program and a tornado awareness program and distribute earthquake and tornado preparedness literature to schools, community groups, newspapers and local radio and TV and civic organizations;

3.7. Disseminate all information, completely and without delay, on water levels for streams and rivers and any data pertaining to potential flooding supplied by the Division of Water Resources within the Illinois Department of Transportation to the maximum extent possible;

3.8. Prepare, for issuance by the Mayor, ordinances, proclamations and regulations necessary or appropriate in coping with disasters;

3.9. Cooperate with the federal, State and county governments and any public or private agency or entity in achieving any purpose of this Article and in implementing programs for disaster prevention, preparation, response and recovery;

3.10. Initiate and coordinate planning for:

3.10.1. The establishment and maintenance of an emergency operating center (EOC);

3.10.2. The implementation and maintenance of a warning system to all schools, hospitals, nursing homes, public buildings, business and industrial buildings, etc.;

3.11. Do all other things necessary, incidental or appropriate for the implementation of this Article;

3.12. Coordinate the recruitment of volunteer personnel and agencies to augment personnel and facilities of the City for emergency service purposes;

3.13. Prepare and keep current an emergency operating plan for its geographic boundaries;

3.14. Prepare and distribute to all appropriate officials in written form a clear and complete statement of the emergency responsibilities of all local departments and officials and of the disaster chain of command;

3.15. Perform such other duties as may be provided under the Act or from time to time designated by the City Manager.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)

### 1-8D-7: POWERS OF THE MAYOR AND CITY OFFICIALS

1\. The Mayor and City officials shall have such emergency powers as provided under the law.

2\. The Mayor may exercise the emergency power and authority necessary to fulfill their general powers and duties as defined by law. The judgment of the Mayor shall be the sole criteria necessary to invoke emergency powers provided by this Article. The City Council may convene to perform its legislative and administrative powers as the situation demands, and shall receive reports relative to the emergency service activities. Nothing in this Article shall be construed as abridging or curtailing the powers or restrictions of the Mayor and/or City Council as defined by law.

3\. The City Council may, on recommendation of the City Manager, authorize any purchase or contracts necessary to place the City in a position to combat effectively any disaster, and to protect the public health and safety, protect property and provide emergency assistance to victims in the case of such disaster. In the event of any disaster, the City Manager is authorized, on behalf of the City, to procure such services, supplies, equipment or materials, as may be necessary for such purposes in view of exigency, without formalities normally prescribed by law. If the City Council meets at the time of such disaster, the City Manager shall act subject to the directions and restrictions imposed by the City Council.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8D-8: FINANCING

1\. Funds to prepare for and meet disasters may be made available as from time to time designated by the City Council within budgetary limits.

2\. It is the City Council's intent that the first recourse shall be to funds regularly allocated to the agency. If the Mayor finds that demands placed upon budgeted funds in coping with a particular disaster are unreasonably great, they may make application for funds from the State disaster relief fund. If monies available from the fund are insufficient, and if the Mayor finds that other sources of money to cope with the disaster are not available or are insufficient, they shall issue a call for an immediate session of the City Council for the purpose of enacting ordinances as the Council may deem necessary to transfer and expend monies appropriated for other purposes, or borrow monies from the United States government or other public or private sources. If less than a quorum of the members of the Council is capable of convening in session to enact such ordinances for the transfer, expenditure or loan of such monies, the Mayor is authorized to carry out these decisions until such time as a quorum of the Council can convene.

3\. Nothing contained in this Section shall be construed to limit the Mayor's authority to apply for, administer and expend grants, gifts or payments in aid of disaster mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery.

4\. The City Council may make appropriations for emergency management operations in the manner provided by law for making appropriations for the ordinary expenses of the City.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8D-9: PERSONNEL INJURY OR DEATH COMPENSATION

1\. Naperville Emergency Management Agency personnel who, while engaged in a disaster or disaster training exercise, suffer disease, injury or death shall, for the purposes of benefits under the Workers' Compensation Act or Workers' Occupational Diseases Act only, be deemed to be employees of the state, if: 1) the claimant is duly qualified and enrolled (sworn in) as a volunteer of the Illinois Emergency Management Agency or an emergency management agency accredited by the Illinois Emergency Management Agency, and 2) if the claimant was participating in an actual "disaster" as defined in Section 1-8D-4 of this Article or the exercise participated in was specifically and expressly approved by the Illinois Emergency Management Agency. The Illinois Emergency Management Agency shall use the same criteria for approving an exercise and utilizing State volunteers as required for any political subdivision. The computation of benefits payable under either of these Acts shall be based on the income commensurate with comparable State employees doing the same work or income from the person's regular employment, whichever is greater.

2\. If any person who is entitled to receive benefits through the application of this Section receives in connection with the disease, injury or death giving rise to such entitlement, benefits under an act of congress or federal program, benefits payable under this Section shall be reduced to the extent of the benefits received under that other act or program.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)

### 1-8D-10: LOCAL DISASTER DECLARATIONS

1\. A local disaster may be declared only by the Mayor, or their interim emergency successor. It shall not be continued or renewed for a period in excess of seven (7) days except by or with the consent of the City Council. Any order or proclamation declaring, continuing or terminating a local disaster shall be given prompt and general publicity and shall be filed promptly with the City Clerk.

2\. The effect of a declaration of a local disaster is to activate the emergency operations plan of the City and to authorize the furnishing of aid and assistance thereunder.

3\. A local disaster emergency may not be declared by an official (Fire Chief, Police Chief, etc.) from another jurisdiction who is operating in the City under a mutual aid agreement.

4\. During a local disaster emergency, the Mayor may suspend the provisions of any municipal ordinance prescribing procedures for the conduct of City business, or the orders, rules and regulations of any City agency, if strict compliance with the provisions of any ordinance, rule or regulation would in any way prevent, hinder or delay necessary action in coping with the emergency, as authorized by the "Illinois Emergency Services and Disaster Agency Act of 1988", provided that after the City Council meets at such time, the Mayor shall act subject to the directions and restrictions imposed by the City Council.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8D-11: IMMUNITY

Neither the City, nor, except in cases of negligence or wilful misconduct, the Mayor, the City Manager, or the officers, employees, volunteers, or representatives engaged in any emergency management response or recovery activities, while complying with or attempting to comply with this Article or any rule or regulations promulgated pursuant to this Article is liable for the death of or any injury to persons, or damage to property, as a result of such activity. This Section does not, however, affect the right of any person to receive benefits to which they would otherwise be entitled under this Article under the Workers' Compensation Act or the Workers' Occupational Disease Act, or under any pension law, and this Section does not affect the right of any such person to receive any benefits or compensation under any act of Congress.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 1-8D-12: SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Article or the applications thereof to any person or circumstance be held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect such other provisions or applications of this Article which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this Article are hereby declared to be severable.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)

### 1-8D-13: EMERGENCY TERMINATION OR REDUCTION OF ELECTRICAL SERVICE

When in the judgment of the Mayor or the City Council, as provided herein, a local disaster emergency requires the termination or reduction of electrical service, the Mayor or the City Council shall forthwith declare in writing the existence of the emergency condition and order the termination or reduction.

(Ord. No. 04-165, 10-5-2004)
