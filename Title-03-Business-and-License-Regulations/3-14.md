### 3-14-1: DEFINITIONS

CODE: The Municipal Code of the City of Naperville, Illinois.

TAX: Any pecuniary obligation imposed by the City on individuals or entities to support City government that is not a permit or other fee (excepting the infrastructure maintenance fee), is not collected or administered by a department or agency of the State of Illinois, or is not a tax on real property under the Illinois Property Tax Code\*, including, but not limited to, any of the following taxes that may now, or in the future be collected or administered by the City:

Amusement tax Cigarette tax Coin operated games and devices tax Foreign fire insurance tax Hotel/motel tax Infrastructure maintenance fee

Motor fuel tax Real estate transfer tax Telecommunications tax

Utility tax.

TAX ADMINISTRATOR: The Finance Director of the City of Naperville or the Finance Director's designee. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001; amd. Ord. 02-202, 9-17-2002)

### 3-14-2: APPLICATION OF PAYMENTS

1\. Notwithstanding any provision in this Code to the contrary, any payment designated by a taxpayer as payment of a particular tax liability shall be applied to that taxpayer's liabilities pertaining to the particular tax as follows: first to the interest accrued on outstanding tax liabilities; second to the outstanding tax liability; third to any penalties due on outstanding tax liabilities; and fourth to current tax liabilities. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)

> \* 35 ILCS 200/ 1-1 et seq.

### 3-14-3: INTEREST ON OVERDUE PAYMENTS

1\. Notwithstanding any provision in this Code to the contrary, any tax not paid on the date due shall accrue interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) of the amount due for each month or part of a month the tax remains unpaid. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)

### 3-14-4: DETERMINATION OF TAX LIABILITY

1\. To collect overdue taxes, a determination notice shall be sent to the taxpayer by registered or certified mail no more than four (4) years after the end of the calendar year when the tax was due; however, if less than seventy-five percent (75%) of the tax due was paid, a determination notice may be sent no more than six (6) years after the end of the calendar year when the tax was due.

2\. All determinations of tax liability shall include notice of the following:

```text
        2.1.        Reason for tax liability; and
```

2.2. The amount of tax, interest and penalty due on the date of the determination and information on how interest and penalties accrue; and

2.3. Procedure for appeal; and

2.4. Information regarding the right of the City to audit the taxpayer's books and records for a particular period and its obligation to refund overpayments. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)

### 3-14-5: APPEALS

1\. Any taxpayer may within forty-five (45) days of the date of receipt of a determination of tax liability or at any time before a current tax becomes due seek a hearing before the Tax Administrator to appeal the tax liability.

2\. For reasonable cause shown in writing by the taxpayer and full payment of the amount of tax protested plus accrued interest due, the Tax Administrator shall extend the 45-day period for appeal after a determination has been issued for up to an additional forty-five (45) days. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)

### 3-14-6: CREDITS AND REFUNDS

1\. Notwithstanding any provision in this Code to the contrary, taxpayers may, in writing, claim a credit or refund for payment of taxes, interest or penalties paid in error for up to four (4) years after the end of the calendar year in which the payment was made. Claims must include dates, amount paid, and an explanation of the error that occurred. The Tax Administrator shall determine whether a credit or refund is justified and shall respond to the taxpayer in writing within thirty (30) days of receipt of the claim. Tax amounts paid in error shall be returned plus five percent (5%) interest. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)

### 3-14-7: VOLUNTARY DISCLOSURE

1\. A taxpayer who has not received a written notice of an audit or determination of a tax liability may seek disclosure of tax liability by applying to the Tax Administrator in writing for voluntary disclosure of the taxpayer's liability pertaining to a particular tax and agreeing to pay within ninety (90) days of the date of the application, the tax due plus one percent (1%) interest per month for all periods prior to filing the application but not more than four (4) years prior thereto. The taxpayer shall be liable for any underpaid taxes and accrued interest during the four-year time period but, except for the taxes and interest due under this Section, the taxpayer shall not be liable for any taxes imposed prior to the date of the application for voluntary disclosure. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)

### 3-14-8: LIENS

1\. In the first month of each year the Tax Administrator shall cause all liens against taxpayers to be reviewed. The City shall at its sole expense release improper liens, correct the taxpayer's credit record and correct any public disclosure of said liens. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)

### 3-14-9: PENALTIES

1\. A penalty of five percent (5%) of the amount of tax due shall be charged against any taxpayer whose tax payment is not made on or before the date the tax is due.

2\. A penalty of twenty-five percent (25%) of the amount of tax due for the period in which a report or return is required to be filed shall be charged against any taxpayer who fails to file, before the date that a determination of liability is issued, a report or return for that period.

3\. Any penalty may be abated by the Tax Administrator if reasonable cause for nonpayment or failure to file is shown. (Ord. 00-209, 12-5-2000, eff. 1-1-2001)
