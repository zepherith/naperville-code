# 3-2 GENERAL LICENSE REGULATIONS

### 3-2-1: APPLICABILITY OF PROVISIONS

Except as otherwise provided, the provisions of this Chapter shall apply to the issuance of all licenses and permits required by the ordinances, rules and regulations of the City.

(Ord. No. 82-26, 3-1-82)

### 3-2-2: PERSONS SUBJECT TO LICENSE

Whenever in this Code a license is required for the maintenance, operation or conduct of any business or establishment, or for doing business or engaging in any activity or occupation, any person or corporation shall be subject to the requirement if by themselves or through an agent, employee or partner, they hold themselves forth as being engaged in the business or occupation; solicits patronage therefor, actively or passively; or, performs or attempts to perform any part of such business or occupation in the City.

(Ord. No. 82-26, 3-1-82; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 3-2-3: APPLICATIONS FOR LICENSE

Applications for all licenses and permits required by ordinance shall be made in writing to the City Clerk in the absence of provision to the contrary. Each application shall state the name of the applicant, the permit or license desired, the location to be used, if any, the time covered and the fee to be paid; and each application shall contain such additional information as may be needed for the proper guidance of the City officials in the issuing of the permit or license applied for.

(Ord. No. 82-26, 3-1-82)

### 3-2-4: CONTENTS OF LICENSES AND PERMITS

1\. Forms: Forms for all licenses and permits, and applications therefor, shall be prepared and kept on file by the City Clerk.

2\. Signatures: Each license or permit issued shall bear the signatures of the Mayor and the City Clerk in the absence of any provision to the contrary.

### 3-2-5: INVESTIGATION OF APPLICANTS

Upon receipt of an application for a license or permit where ordinances of the City necessitate an inspection or investigation before the issuance of such permit, the City Clerk shall refer such application to the proper officer for making such investigation within forty-eight (48) hours of the time of such receipt. The officer charged with the duty of making the investigation or inspection shall make a report thereon, favorable or otherwise, within ten (10) days after receiving the application or a copy thereof.

(Ord. No. 82-26, 3-1-82)

### 3-2-6: FEES

In the absence of provision to the contrary, all fees and charges for licenses or permits shall be paid in advance at the time application therefor is made to the Finance Director. Except as otherwise provided, all license fees shall become a part of the corporate fund.

(Ord. No. 82-26, 3-1-82; amd. 1984 Code)

### 3-2-7: LICENSE TERMS

All annual licenses shall be due on July 1 of each year and shall terminate on June 30 of the following year, where no provision to the contrary is made. The City Clerk shall mail to all annual licensees of the City a statement of the time of expiration of the license held by the licensee three (3) weeks prior to the date of such expiration. Provided, that a failure to send out such notice, or the failure of the licensee to receive it shall not excuse the licensee from a failure to obtain a new license, or a renewal thereof, nor shall it be a defense in an action from operation without a license.

### 3-2-8: BUILDING AND PERMITS

1\. General: No license shall be issued for the conduct of any business, and no permit shall be issued for any thing or act, if the premises and building to be used for the purpose do not fully comply with the requirements of the City. No such license or permit shall be issued for the conduct of any business or performance of any act which would involve a violation of the Zoning Ordinance or Municipal Code of the City.

2\. Change of Location: The location of any licensed business or occupation or for any permitted act, may be changed, provided ten (10) days' notice thereof is given to the City Clerk, in the absence of any provision to the contrary; provided that the building and zoning requirements of the City are complied with.

### 3-2-9: POSTING LICENSES AND TAGS

1\. It shall be the duty of any person conducting a licensed business in the City to keep their license posted in a prominent place on the premises used for such business at all times.

2\. Whenever the number of vehicles used is the basis of a license fee, the Finance Director shall furnish each licensee with a tag or sticker which shall be posted in a conspicuous place on each such vehicle while it is in use.

(Ord. No. 82-26, 3-1-82; amd. 1984 Code; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 3-2-10: CITY LOGO, USE OF

1\. License Required: No person shall use or display the City logo without first obtaining a license therefor in accordance with this Section from the City Clerk.

2\. License Fee: The annual license fee hereunder shall be thirty-five dollars (\$35.00), except not-for-profit organizations which shall pay an annual license fee of five dollars (\$5.00). License applications shall be forwarded to such board, commission or committee as the City Council may designate, which shall recommend whether a license shall be issued and the conditions under which such license should be granted. No license shall be granted except under the terms of a license agreement executed by the applicant and agreed by the City Council.

3\. License Term: The term of a license issued in accordance with this Section shall be July 1 to June 30 of the next succeeding license year.

(Ord. No. 82-26, 3-1-82)

### 3-2-11: INSPECTIONS

1\. Any officer or employee of the City who is charged with the duty of inspecting premises used for or in connection with the operation of a licensed business or occupation, when an inspection is for the purpose of securing compliance with any ordinance provision, or to detect a violation thereof, shall first request the permission of the owner or occupant to enter the premises. If permission is denied, the officer or employee shall apply, with the assistance of the Corporation Counsel, to the appropriate court for a search warrant.

2\. Whenever an analysis of any commodity or material is reasonably necessary to secure conformance with any ordinance provision, or to detect a violation thereof, the investigating City official or employee shall request the owner or possessor of the commodity or material to provide them with a sufficient sample thereof. If the request is denied, the officer or employee shall, with the assistance of the Corporation Counsel, apply to the appropriate court for a search warrant.

3\. In addition to any other penalty which may be provided, the City Manager may revoke the license of any licensed proprietor of any licensed business who refuses to comply with a search warrant issued by a court of competent jurisdiction. However, no license may be revoked until notice of the revocation is served on the licensee, personally or by certified mail, at the business address stated in the license application. Within seven (7) days of the notice of revocation, the licensee may request an informal hearing before the City Manager, and at that time, may present evidence showing that the license should not be revoked. The City Manager shall then affirm or vacate the revocation.

(Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 3-2-12: REVOCATION OF LICENSES OR PERMITS

Any license, except for the sale of intoxicating liquors, or permit may be revoked by the City Manager for cause, at any time. Evidence that the licensee or permittee has violated any ordinance relating to the license or permit, the subject matter of the license or permit, or the premises occupied, shall constitute sufficient cause for revocation. Notice of the revocation shall be served on the licensee or permittee, personally or by certified mail, at the address stated in the license or permit application. Within seven (7) days of the date of the notice of revocation, the licensee or permittee may request an informal hearing before the City Manager, and at that time, may present evidence showing that the license or permit should not be revoked. The City Manager shall then affirm or vacate the revocation.

(1984 Code)
