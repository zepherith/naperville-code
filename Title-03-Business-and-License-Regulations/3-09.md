# 3-9 AMPLIFIERS

### 3-9-1: AMPLIFICATION

1\. Except as otherwise provided in this Section, it shall be unlawful to maintain or operate any loudspeaker or amplifier connected with any radio, microphone or other device by which sounds are magnified and made heard over any public street or public place in the City without having first secured a permit.

Any person or organization desiring a permit for the use or operation of such device shall file an application with the City Clerk, upon a form provided by the City Clerk, setting forth the name and address of the applicant, the name of the owner of such device, the name and address of the person or persons who will use the device, the date upon which it is intended to be used, the exact location the loudspeaker or amplifier is to be used, the hours of use for such loudspeaker or amplifier, and other such information as may be prescribed.

Nonresidential permits shall be issued daily or monthly, and only one permit shall be held at any time by any person or for any location.

Residential permits shall be issued daily. Monthly residential permits shall be issued by the City Council.

All amplification requiring a permit shall be issued in accordance with this Section, and shall fully comply with this Code and Illinois State law.

2\. Restaurants, restaurants and taverns, and taverns, as defined in this Title, which hold a valid local retail liquor license issued by the City and which operate within areas zoned B4 as provided by Title 6 of this Code shall be allowed to amplify recorded music, brief business related announcements, and live music/entertainment and shall be exempt from the permitting provisions set forth in this Section subject to the following restrictions:

2.1. All amplification, excluding live music/entertainment, shall only occur during times as the restaurant, restaurant and tavern, or tavern is actually open and operating and shall not occur after twelve thirty o'clock (12:30) a.m. on Monday through Friday, and after one thirty o'clock (1:30) a.m. on Saturday and Sunday, nor before the hour of nine o'clock (9:00) a.m. of any day.

2.2. Amplification of live music/entertainment shall only occur during times as the restaurant, restaurant and tavern, or tavern is actually open and operating and shall not occur after twelve o'clock (12:00) midnight Central Standard Time on Monday through Friday, and after one o'clock (1:00) a.m. on Saturday and Sunday, nor before the hour of nine o'clock (9:00) a.m. of any day.

2.3. Such amplification shall not be audible more than three hundred (300) feet beyond the property line of the business.

3\. Businesses other than those set forth in this Section which are located within areas zoned B4 and B5 provided by Title 6 of this Code shall be allowed to amplify recorded music and brief business related announcements and shall be exempt from the permitting provisions set forth in this Section subject to the following restrictions:

3.1. Such amplification may only occur during times the establishment is actually open and operating and shall not occur after eleven o'clock (11:00) p.m. on Sunday through Thursday, and after twelve o'clock (12:00) midnight on Friday and Saturday, nor before the hour of nine o'clock (9:00) a.m. of any day.

3.2. Such amplification shall not be audible more than twenty-five (25) feet beyond the property line of the business.

3.3. Live music and other live entertainment require a permit and are subject to the provisions of this Section.

(Ord. 08-086, 5-6-2008)

### 3-9-2: PERMIT FEES

The fee for permits to be granted under this Chapter shall be five dollars (\$5.00) per day or twenty-five dollars (\$25.00) per month. The City Council, by motion, may waive the fee required.

(Ord. 08-086, 5-6-2008)

### 3-9-3: PERMIT ISSUANCE

1\. Permits Issued By City Clerk:

1.1. Nonresidential Permit: A nonresidential permit for use, operation or employment of any such loudspeaker or amplification device at any location of which there are no other residences on the permittee's property and there are no residentially zoned properties within two hundred (200) feet measured from the nearest property line of the permittee's property to the nearest property line of the residential property. Such permit shall permit the use of any such loudspeaker or amplification device until the hour of nine o'clock (9:00) p.m. on Sunday through Thursday, and until the hour of ten o'clock (10:00) p.m. on Friday or Saturday, and after the hour of nine o'clock (9:00) a.m. of any day.

(Ord. 08-086, 5-6-2008)

1.2. Residential Permit: A residential permit for use, operation or employment of any loudspeaker or amplification device between the hours of twelve o'clock (12:00) noon to eight o'clock (8:00) p.m. Sunday through Thursday, and between the hours of twelve o'clock (12:00) noon to ten o'clock (10:00) p.m. Friday and Saturday.

(Ord. 09-050, 5-5-2009)

2\. Permits Issued By The City Clerk Are Subject To The Following Restrictions:

2.1. Hospitals And Churches: No permittee shall use, operate or employ any such device within a radius of two (2) blocks from any hospital or within the radius of two (2) blocks from any church while funeral services are being held there.

2.2. Profanity Or Misrepresentation: No permittee shall cause or permit to be emanated or emitted from any such device any lewd, obscene, profane or indecent language or sounds, or any false representation of any matter, product or project advertised thereby, the sale of which is prohibited by any law, ordinance or statute.

2.3. Moving Vehicles: No loudspeakers or amplifiers shall be used from moving vehicles except as expressly provided within this Chapter.

2.4. Audibility Limit: No amplified sound may be emitted from a property such that it may be heard two hundred (200) feet beyond the property line of the property from which said sound originates.

(Ord. 08-086, 5-6-2008)

### 3-9-4: CITY COUNCIL APPROVAL REQUIRED

1\. The following requires City Council approval:

1.1. Amplification for nonresidential uses located within two hundred (200) feet of a residential zoning district (e.g., R1, R1A, R1B, R2, R3, R3A and R4) provided in Title 6 of this Code measured from the nearest property line of the nonresidential property to the nearest property line of the residential property, excluding properties zoned B4 which are exempt per this Chapter.

1.2. Amplification for nonresidential uses with residential uses on the same property, excluding properties zoned B4 which are exempt per this Chapter.

1.3. Amplification for properties zoned B4 which exceed the restrictions found in Subsection 3-9-1.2 of this Chapter.

1.4. Residential amplification for a period of thirty (30) days or more.

(Ord. 08-086, 5-6-2008)

1.5. Residential and nonresidential amplification associated with a special event that do not comply with Subsection 3-9-3.1 of this Chapter. Special event amplification permits shall be subject to any lawful conditions or restrictions established by the City Council and shall be valid until ten o'clock (10:00) p.m. for the date issued. No extensions will be granted.

(Ord. 09-050, 5-5-2009)

### 3-9-5: PENALTY

Any person violating any provision of this Chapter shall be fined not less than fifty dollars (\$50.00) nor more than five hundred dollars (\$500.00) for each offense, and a separate offense shall be deemed committed on each day during or on which a violation occurs or continues.

Any permit holder violating any provision of this Chapter shall also be subject to immediate revocation of permit without prior notice. No person or organization named on a revoked permit as the applicant or user shall be eligible for issuance of another amplifier permit, nor shall an amplifier permit be issued for use at a location which was the subject of a revoked permit within a period of ninety (90) days after revocation of the first permit. The holder of a revoked permit shall have the right of appeal in accordance with the provisions of Section 3-2-12 of this Title.

(Ord. 08-086, 5-6-2008)

### 3-9-6: EXCEPTIONS

1\. This Chapter shall not apply to the following:

1.1. Radios: Radios in homes or in private pleasure vehicles, when the same are operated in such manner as not to be audible at a distance of fifty (50) feet from such home or vehicle.

1.2. Parades: Noise devices, bands or other musical devices used in any public parade or procession which is operated under a permit in accordance with the ordinances of the City.

(Ord. 08-086, 5-6-2008)

1.3. Recreational Activities: The authorized use of subdivision pools for swim meets and recreational facilities within the property of schools, colleges, and public parks between the hours of six o'clock (6:00) a.m. and ten o'clock (10:00) p.m.

(Ord. 09-050, 5-5-2009)
