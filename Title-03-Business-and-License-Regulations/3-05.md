### 3-5A-1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE

The purpose of this Article is to register and bond any person as an Electrical Contractor; and to establish fees for same. (Ord. No. 97-06, 2-4-1997; Ord. No. 21-014, § 2, 2-16-2021)

### 3-5A-3: REGISTRATION REQUIRED

1\. Securing Permits: Only an Electrical Contractor properly registered with the City of Naperville may secure electrical permits and furnish electrical equipment and components for installation in the City of Naperville by a Licensed Electrician in the employ of the Electrical Contractor, or to be installed under the supervision of the Licensed Electrician.

1.1. The Electrical Contractor registration application shall include:

1.1.1. The name of the applicant;

1.1.2. The name of the applicant's place of business, whether individual, corporation or partnership (if partnership it shall include the names of all partners);

1.1.3. The address of the applicant's place of business, and the name, address and position of all licensed electricians in the Electrical Contractor's employ; and

1.1.4. The applicant's residential address.

1.2. The annual registration fee for an Electrical Contractor shall be fifty dollars ($50.00).

1.3. Every person desiring to register as an Electrical Contractor shall provide a corporate surety bond in the penal sum of ten thousand dollars ($10 ,000.00) payable to the City of Naperville for the use of the City of Naperville and any person interested, conditioned upon the applicant's full compliance with all rules, regulations and ordinances of the City of Naperville pertaining to the installation or alteration of electric wiring, apparatus or equipment, and also conditioned to indemnify the City of Naperville and persons interested from any loss, liability or damage arising out of performance of the applicant's work. The corporate surety bond shall be in effect and run concurrent with the term of the annual registration from January 1 through December 31 of the same calendar year.

1.4. All applicants shall supply a copy of the Electrical Contractor's certificate of liability insurance naming the City of Naperville as the certificate holder.

2\. Licensed Electrician Status Required: If the person seeking to register as an Electrical Contractor is an individual acting as a sole proprietor, they must be a Licensed Electrician. If the individual seeking to register is a corporation, partnership, group or association, it must have as an officer, partner, or member, an individual who is a Licensed Electrician.

3\. Notice Of Discharge: Whenever a Licensed Electrician shall leave or be discharged from the employ of any Electrical Contractor registered with the City of Naperville under this Section, notice in writing thereof shall be given within five (5) days by both the Electrical Contractor and the Licensed Electrician to the Naperville City Clerk. If said discharge of a Licensed Electrician leaves an Electrical Contractor without a Licensed Electrician on staff, then the registration of such Electrical Contractor shall, without further order or action by the electrical inspector, automatically be suspended until the Electrical Contractor employs a Licensed Electrician. (Ord.

No. 97-06, 2-4-1997; Ord. No. 12-113, § 1, 9-18-2012; Ord. No. 21-014, § 2, 2-16-2021; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 3-5A-4: LICENSE REQUIRED

No person may, within the City of Naperville, install or alter equipment and electrical appliances for the utilization of electricity supplied by light, heat or power not including radio apparatus or equipment for wireless reception of sounds and signals, and not including apparatus, conductors and other equipment installed for or by public utilities including common carriers, which are under the jurisdiction of the Illinois Commerce Commission for use in their operation as public utilities without being a Licensed Electrician. (Ord. No. 97-06, 2-4-1997; Ord. No. 12-113, § 1, 9-18-2012; Ord. No. 21-014, § 2, 2-16-2021)

### 3-5A-5: RENEWAL OF REGISTRATION

The term of the annual registration issued in accordance with this Article shall be January 1 through December 31 of the same calendar year. The corporate surety bond required by Subsection 3-5A-1.3. of this Article shall be in effect and run concurrent with the term of the annual registration provided for herein.

Every Electrical Contractor who has received a registration shall be entitled for renewal upon making a proper and complete application to the Naperville City Clerk, including paying the fifty-dollar ($50.00) application renewal fee. (Ord. No. 97-06, 2-4-1997; Ord. No. 12-113, § 1, 9-18-2012; Ord. No. 21-014, § 2, 2-16-2021)

### 3-5A-6: REVOCATION OF A REGISTRATION

1\. Revocation: An electrical contractor's registration may be revoked under the following conditions:

1.1. Noncomplianc e with any of the provisions of this Article or any other ordinances of the City applying to electrical work.

1.2. Performing, engaging in, or providing electrical work without obtaining a building permit as required by Section 5-2A-1 of this Code.

1.3. Failure to execute a bond in accordance with this Article or failure to maintain such bond in full force and effect during the entire term of the electrical contractor's registration.

1.4. The performance of any act or any work without regard for the health, safety and welfare of the person, firm or corporation for whom such work is performed.

2\. Hearing To Revoke Registration:

2.1. Upon the Chief Building Code Official's preliminary determination that cause may exist to revoke a registration issued pursuant to the provisions of this Chapter, the Code Official shall serve written notice to the registrant of the alleged violations and that a hearing will be held to determine whether the registration should be revoked.

2.2. The written notice of intent to revoke a license shall be served by certified mail, return receipt requested, and standard U.S. Postal Service at the address shown on the registration and shall contain a general statement of the relevant facts which support the revocation, the date, time and place of the hearing to determine whether the registration should be revoked and the consequences of failing to appear at the hearing.

2.3. At the hearing, the registrant may appear in person or with an attorney and may bring witnesses to testify on their behalf. Informal rules of evidence shall apply at the hearing.

2.4. Within ten (10) days of the hearing, the city manager or their designee shall issue a written decision as to whether the registration should be revoked and the reasons therefor.

### 3-5A-7: HOMEOWNER S, EXCEPTIONS

Bona fide homeowners will be permitted to alter, modify or install electric wiring devices or appliances in their own home, where they reside, by obtaining a homeowner's electrical registration, provided the homeowner successfully passes a written examination test supervised by the Transportation, Engineering and Development Department. Homeowners who obtain permission of the Chief Building Official by test will be required to file with the City Clerk an application for a permit as a homeowner desiring to do their own wiring upon a form provided for that purpose, accompanied with payment of the handling and processing fee of twenty-five dollars ($25.00). No surety bond or certificate of liability insurance shall be required of a homeowner for the issuance of a homeowner's permit. (Ord. No. 97-06, 2-4-1997; Ord. No. 12-113, § 1, 9-18-2012; Ord. ### 3-5C-1: LICENSE REQUIRED, APPLICATION

No person shall construct, replace or repair any public sidewalk or apron of a driveway in the City without a license therefor issued in accordance with this Article. Applications for a license under this Article shall be made to the City Clerk and shall include the following information and such other information as may reasonably be required:

1\. Name, address and business location of the applicant.

2\. If a corporation, the names and addresses of the president and secretary of the corporation.

3\. Name of the person(s) responsible for the construction, replacement or repair to be performed.

4\. Statement that the applicant is familiar with and agrees to abide by the provisions of the ordinances, rules and regulations of the City pertaining to sidewalk construction, repair or replacement.

5\. Evidence of the applicant's competence or proficiency in sidewalk construction. (Ord. 82-26, 3-1-1982; Ord. No. 12-113, § 3, 9-18-2012)

### 3-5C-2: LICENSE FEE, TERM

The annual license fee under this Article shall be thirty dollars ($30.00), The term of a license issued in accordance herewith shall be January 1 to December 31. (Ord. 82-26, 3-1-1982; Ord. No. 12-113, § 3, 9-18-2012)
