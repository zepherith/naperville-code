# 3-16 COMMERCIAL QUADRICYLCES

SECTION:\
3-16-1: Purpose\
3-16-2: Definitions\
3-16-3: Operation

### 3-16-1: PURPOSE

The purpose of this Chapter is to provide for the safety of the public and preserve the character of the City of Naperville by prohibiting the operation of commercial quadricycles on public property, including streets, highways, and thoroughfares in the City of Naperville. (Ord. No. 18-038, § 1, 4-17-2018)

### 3-16-2: DEFINITIONS

COMMERCIAL QUADRICYCLE: A device or vehicle that satisfies all of the following: (a) has fully operative pedals for propulsion entirely by human power; (b) has at least four (4) wheels and is operated in a manner similar to a bicycle; (c) has at least six (6) seats for passengers; (d) is designated to be occupied by a driver and powered either by passengers providing pedal power to the drive train of the vehicle or by a motor capable of propelling the vehicle in the absence of human power; and (e) is used for commercial purposes. (Ord. No. 18-038, § 1, 4-17-2018)
