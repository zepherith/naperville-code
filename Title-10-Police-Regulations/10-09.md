ARTICLES: applied numbers, letters, characters or markings, or other unique features that serve to distinguish it from any other similar article and can be used to establish ownership of the article.

JEWELRY: Decorative objects made for people to wear on their body such as but not limited to rings, necklaces, earrings, watches, and bracelets.

PAWN: Something given as security for a loan.

PAWNBROKER: Every individual or business entity which lends money on the deposit or pledge of physically delivered personal property, other than property the ownership of which is subject to a legal dispute, securities, printed evidence of indebtedness or printed evidence of ownership of the personal property, or who deals in the purchase of such property on the condition of re-selling the property.

PAWNER: One who gives or deposits jewelry, precious metals or precious stones as security for payment of a loan.

PRECIOUS METALS: Articles consisting primarily of the elements gold (chemical symbol

Au), silver (chemical symbol Ag), platinum (chemical symbol Pt) or palladium (chemical symbol Pd), but not including articles merely plated with these metals or articles similar in color to these metals but not actually consisting of them.

PRECIOUS STONES: Includes but is not limited to diamonds, emeralds, rubies, sapphires, opals, onyx, jade, amethyst and pearls.

SECONDHAND DEALER: Every owner, operator or employee engaged in the primary business of the purchase, sale, trade, barter or consignment of secondhand precious metals, precious stones or jewelry.

TRANSACTION: The receipt of precious metals, jewelry or precious stones by pawnbrokers or secondhand dealers through pawn, purchase, barter, consignment or trade. (Ord. No. 14-076, § 1, 6-3-2014)

### 10-9-2: PROHIBITED ACTS

1\. No pawnbroker or secondhand dealer shall operate within the City of Naperville without first registering with the City Clerk as provided for in this Chapter.

2\. No pawnbroker or secondhand dealer shall operate in violation of the terms and conditions of this Chapter.

2.1. Pawnbrokers or secondhand dealers shall report all transactions through the authorized online database in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter. In the event of a change to the authorized online database, the City will notify all pawnbrokers and secondhand dealers of the change within a reasonable time prior to such change.

2.2. All transactions shall be reported to the authorized online database within twenty-four

(24) hours of the transaction or the next business day. Every transaction shall be reported separately, and not in aggregate. Precious metals or jewelry brought in to a pawnbroker or secondhand dealer by an individual for sale, barter, trade, or pawn at different times on the same date shall be considered a separate transaction and shall be repo rted separately. (Ord. No. 14-076, § 1, 6-3-2014)

### 10-9-3: AUTHORIZED ONLINE DATABASE REPORTING

1\. Equipment Requirements:

1.1. Every pawnbroker and secondhand dealer shall operate and maintain a computer system with internet access and ability to upload photographs to report all required transactions as provided for in this Chapter to the authorized online database.

1.2. The failure or malfunction of such equipment on the part of the pawnbroker or secondhand dealer shall not exempt the pawnbroker or secondhand dealer business from the recording/reporting requirements as provided for in this Chapter. Notice of computer failure shall be immediately reported to the Naperville Police Department.

1.3. In the event the authorized online database is not operational, written records of all transactions shall be kept until such time as the database is operational.

1.3.1. The written records shall contain all information required under this Chapter and shall be reported/uploaded to the authorized online database as soon as practicable after the database is functional.

1.3.2. No such written records shall be erased, mutilated or changed. Written records shall be open and available for review upon request by any police officer during regular business hours.

2\. Reporting Requirements: For each transaction involving the rece ipt of precious metals, precious stones or jewelry, the pawnbroker or secondhand dealer shall record or report the following information:

2.1. The name, current address, date of birth, and a digital copy of the current government issued photo ID of the pawner or seller; date and time of the transaction; and description and photograph of each article; and

### 10-9-4: WAITING PERIOD

1\. No articles received on deposit or pawn by any pawnbroker or secondhand dealer shall be redeemed or removed from the registered location within forty-eight (48) hours after the initial receipt.

2\. No pawnbroker/secondhand dealer shall sell, trade or barter, or melt, crush/compact, destroy, or otherwise dispose of any individually identifiable precious metals or jewelry article within ten (10) days of the transaction or the reporting to the authorized online database. (Ord. No. 14-076, § 1, 6-3-2014)

### 10-9-5: PROHIBITED TRANSACTIONS

No pawnbroker or secondhand dealer, in the course of conducting their business, shall receive any articles for pawn, pledge, advancement of money, loan, resale, or personal use from:

1\. Minors who are under eighteen (18) years of age;

2\. Any person appearing to be intoxicated or under the influence of drugs or alcohol;

3\. Any person known to have been convicted of theft, residential burglary, burglary, home invasion, vehicular invasion, armed robbery, robbery or electronic fencing, or similar crime in the last five (5) years;

4\. Any person presenting a serial numbered precious metals and/or jewelry article from which the serial number has been scratched off or obliterated;

5\. Any person without a valid picture ID; and

6\. Any person when the items appear to have been stolen.

In the event that any of the above enumerated persons shall attempt to conduct a transaction, a representative of the pawnbroker or secondhand dealer shall immediately notify the Naperville Police Department of the attempted prohibited transaction as soon as possible. (Ord. No. 14-076, § 1, 6-3-2014; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 10-9-6: INSPECTION OF RECORDS

Upon request, pawnbrokers and secondhand dealers shall provide to the Naperville Police Department access to records of transactions to determine compliance with this Chapter. (Ord. No. 14-076, § 1, 6-3-2014)

### 10-9-7: REGISTRATION

All pawnbrokers and secondhand dealers shall register with the City Clerk. Said registration shall include the full name, address and telephone number of the applicant if an individual, and if a corporation the name, address and telephone number of each of its officers. Such application shall also set out the location of such business. All change of addresses of pawnbrokers or secondhand dealers registered under this Section shall be reported to the City Clerk's Office within twenty-four (24) hours of moving to their new location. (Ord. No. 14-076, § 1, 6-3-2014)

### 10-9-8: PENALTY PROVISIONS

Violations of this chapter shall be punishable by a fine of five hundred dollars ($500.00) for a first offense, and one thousand dollars ($1,000.00) for any subsequent offenses. Each day the violation continues shall be a separate offense. Continued non-compliance with this Chapter shall be considered a threat to the health, safety and welfare of the City and may result in the City seeking further legal action against the pawnbroker or secondhand dealer. (Ord. No. 14-076, § 1, 6-3-2014)
