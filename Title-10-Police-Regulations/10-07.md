# 10-7 FIREWORKS; EXPLOSIVES

### 10-7-1: FIREWORKS

1\. It shall be unlawful to sell, store, possess, discharge or set off any fireworks or to give any pyrotechnic display in the City, except in connection with public exhibitions as is herein provided.

It shall be unlawful for any person to sell, possess, offer for sale or keep for the purpose of selling any fireworks in the City at any time.

2\. No public exhibition of fireworks or pyrotechnics shall be given unless a permit therefor is first secured. All such public displays shall be under the supervision of a competent person and shall be superintended by the Fire Chief or designated representative.

3\. Permits for a public exhibition of fireworks or pyrotechnics shall be approved by the Fire Chief or designee except for the Independence Day fireworks display or any fireworks display associated with a special event permit. These fireworks display permits require City Council approval.

4\. The fee for a fireworks permit is eighty-six dollars (\$86.00). The fee for a fireworks site inspection and engine standby is two hundred forty-six dollars (\$246.00).

(1960 Code § 23.401; amd. 1984 Code; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022; Ord. No. 22-123, § 1, 11-15-2022)

### 10-7-2: EXPLOSIVES

1\. Nitroglycerine, T.N.T.: It shall be unlawful to keep or store any nitroglycerine or the explosive commonly known as T.N.T. in the City in any quantities, excepting for medicinal purposes or laboratory purposes, and for such purposes no more than one-quarter-ounce shall be stored in any one building or premises.

2\. Storage Rules: All explosives must be stored or kept in accordance with the rules of the State Fire Marshal, subject to the provisions of this Section.

(1960 Code § 23.402)

### 10-7-3: PENALTY

Any person violating any provision of this Chapter shall be fined not less than ten dollars (\$10.00) nor more than two hundred dollars (\$200.00) for each offense; and a separate offense shall be deemed committed on each day during or on which a violation occurs or continues.

(1960 Code § 23.404)
