# 10-1 CIVIL EMERGENCIES

SECTION:\
10-1-1: Definitions\
10-1-2: Declaration of Civil Emergency\
10-1-3: Ordering Curiew\
10-1-4: Police Assistance\
10-1-5: Penalty

### 10-1-1: DEFINITIONS

CIVIL EMERGENCY: Shall be interpreted to mean and include the following:

1\. A riot, or any unlawful assembly or gathering of three (3) or more persons, characterized by the use of force or violence upon persons or property, or threatened or obvious intended use of force or violence upon persons or property accompanied by immediate power to execute, acting together or in conjunction with each other, without authority of law, which has or could result in injury to persons or property, or jeopardize the public peace, safety and welfare.

2\. Any natural disaster, or human-made calamity, including flood, conflagration, cyclone, tornado, earthquake, explosion or bombing, within the corporate limits of the City or within the area adjacent to the City, resulting in the death or injury of persons or in the damage or destruction of property to such an extent that extraordinary measures must be taken for the protection and preservation of the public peace, safety and welfare within the City.

CURFEW: A prohibition against any person, or persons, standing, sitting, lying, loitering, walking, running or being within or upon any vehicle, in or upon any alley, street, highway, public property, or vacant premises in any geographical area within the corporate limits of the City, excepting persons officially designated to duty with reference to a civil emergency. (Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 10-1-2: DECLARATION OF CIVIL EMERGENCY

When, in the judgment of the Mayor and the Chief of Police of the City, a civil emergency as defined herein is deemed to exist in the City, the Mayor shall forthwith proclaim, in writing, the existence of a civil emergency in the City. Public notice of such delaration shall be given immediately by all available means as directed by the Mayor.

### 10-1-3: ORDERING CURFEW

After the proclamation by the Mayor of the existence of a civil emergency in the City, the Mayor is hereby authorized to establish, by order a curiew as herein defined, applicable to such geographical areas of the City, or to the City as a whole, if the Mayor deems it advisable, and which shall remain in force and effect during such hours of each day or night as the Mayor deems necessary for the protection and preservation of the public peace, safety and welfare.

After proclamation by the Mayor of the existence of a civil emergency, the Mayor is hereby also authorized, for the purpose of the protection and preservation of the public peace, safety and welfare, to make any one or all of the following orders effective within the City during the period of the curfew:

1\. Closing of all retail liquor stores.

2\. Closing of all taverns.

3\. Prohibiting the sale, serving or giving away of any intoxicating liquor in or by any store, restaurant, private club or other establishment having an alcoholic liquor license issued by the City.

4\. Prohibiting or limiting in any manner the selling or giving away of firearms, ammunition, explosives, gasoline and other flammable liquids or chemicals.

### 10-1-4: POLICE ASSISTANCE

At any time when the Chief of Police is of the opinion that an emergency exists, or is threatened, in the City, by reason of an existing or apparent civil emergency or by reason of any other condition or circumstances, requiring additional police assistance of men and equipment, they are hereby authorized to request such assistance from the Sheriff of DuPage County, and from the Police Departments of area municipalities with which mutual aid agreements have been entered into by the City; and said Chief of Police is hereby authorized and empowered to engage any other additional police assistance which, in their opinion, are necessary under the circumstances. (Ord.

No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 10-1-5: PENALTY

It shall be unlawful for any person to violate the provisions of any curfew or any other order issued by the Mayor under the provisions of Section 10-1-3 hereof, or to fail to obey promptly any reasonable order, direction or command issued by any police officer acting in the performance of their duty or power in the enforcement of the provisions of this Chapter for the preservation of the public peace, health and safety. Any person guilty of such violation shall, upon conviction thereof, be punished by a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100.00) nor more than five hundred dollars ($500.00) for each offense. (Ord. No. 252.68, 12-2-68; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)
