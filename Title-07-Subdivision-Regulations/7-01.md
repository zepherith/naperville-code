### 7-1-1: TITLE AND PURPOSE

This Title shall be known and cited as the SUBDIVISION CONTROL REGULATIONS OF THE CITY OF NAPERVILLE, ILLINOIS.

The purpose of this Title is to establish the requirements and standards regulating the subdivision and platting of land within the corporate limits, and plat approval jurisdiction of the City, for the promotion of the public health, safety, comfort, morals and welfare of the community.

The intent of these regulations is to provide for the orderly and harmonious development of the City and the surrounding areas within the City's planning jurisdiction. (Ord. No. 76-34, 4-20-1976)

### 7-1-2: JURISDICTION

These subdivision control regulations shall be applicable to all lands within the corporate limits at any given time as well as the unincorporated area within one and one-half (11/2) miles of the corporate limits at any given time. Boundary agreements, however, between Naperville and other municipalities, will take precedence in establishing jurisdiction for the unincorporated areas. The provisions of this Title are hereby adopted as part of the comprehensive plan of the City\*. (Ord. No. 76-34, 4-20-1976)

### 7-1-5: IMPROVEMENTS

1\. Before approval of any final plat, the Director of the Department of Community Development or the Assistant Director may require a written development agreement to be prepared by City staff and submitted to and approved by the City Council, to install and construct right-of-way, utility and other public improvements, to pay all required fees, to make all required donations in accordance with the requirements for the ordinances of the City and any other applicable ordinances. The development agreement may state the approximate date when installation of public improvements will commence and a reasonable date for completion of said public improvements. If the development agreement does not otherwise provide a completion date, the improvements shall be completed within three (3) years of the date of final plat approval and recording. A development agreement may be amended to extend the time for commencing and completing public improvements. A development agreement shall be binding upon the successors, heirs and assigns of the subdivider or developer.

2\. Before approval of any final plat or development agreement in such platted subdivision, the subdivider shall guarantee the completion of the public improvements to be installed or constructed in accordance with the development agreement and the ordinances of the City, together with all other commitments by the subdivider by submitting a corporate surety bond or other financing surety in a form to be approved by the City Manager or their designee. For the purposes of this Title, "other financing surety" shall be defined as letters of credit, impound letters, or similar devices made by a sound and reputable financial institution whereby a sum of money is set aside and restricted as payment for the performance of specific obligations by the subdivider and which are irrevocable except by prior notice to the City.

3\. A subdivider may freely assign, transfer or convey property within a platted subdivision, but such assignment, transfer or conveyance shall not affect the principal obligation of the subdivider to perform its obligations under this Title or an executed development agreement without a written amendment to the development agreement. The conveyance of property within a platted subdivision to grantees of such a subdivider shall render such grantees responsible only for performance of a pro rata share of such obligations in the event of default by the subdivider and the surety. (Ord. No. 00-140, 8-15-2000; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 7-1-7: GUARANTEE

1\. A corporate surety bond or other financing surety submitted in accordance with this Section to guarantee the completion of public improvements shall be in an amount equal to one hundred ten percent (110%) of the estimated cost of installing and constructing said public improvements and shall provide as follows:

1.1. An expiration date not less than one year from the date of issuance or an express provision that such surety or bond will not expire prior to completion and City Council acceptance of said public improvements;

1.2. That written notice shall be given by certified or registered mail to the City Clerk of the expiration date, if any, of the surety bond not less than sixty (60) days before said expiration, and that the surety or bond shall not expire absent such notice;

1.3. That failure of the subdivider to complete the public improvements secured by such surety or bond prior to the scheduled completion date shall be considered a default by the subdivider and the issuing institution;

1.4. That failure of the subdivider to submit a new surety or bond in accordance with this Section upon expiration of such surety or bond shall be considered a default by the subdivider and the issuing institution;

1.5. That the surety or bond shall be irrevocable and shall be clearly marked "irrevocable";

1.6. That the surety or bond will not be discharged or reduced by the issuing institution except upon written certificate of the City Clerk that said surety or bond may be discharged or reduced in accordance with this Section and the amount of such discharge or reduction;

1.7. That the issuing institution will pay all attorney fees and other costs reasonably incurred in enforcing collection of such financing surety in the event that the issuer wrongfully fails to honor the City's demand for payment under the terms of such surety or bond.

2\. The requirements of Subsection 7-1-7.1 of this Section may be modified or waived upon motion, resolution or ordinance of the City Council if the Council first determines that the requirements of said Subsection 7-1-7.1 would impose an unreasonable hardship upon the subdivider and that the absence of a surety or bond submitted in accordance with said Subsection will not materially affect the guarantee that the requirements of this Title will be met.

3\. A subdivider may, at its option, submit separate sureties or bonds for each public improvement to be accepted.

4\. A surety or bond submitted in accordance with this Section to guarantee the completion of public improvements may be reduced or partially discharged prior to completion and acceptance of such public improvements by the City Council upon the following showing by the subdivider and the certificate of the City Engineer:

4.1. A sworn statement by the subdivider's engineer of the cost of work performed and labor and materials provided toward the completion of such improvement and estimating the amount and cost of work, labor and material remaining to be performed or provided in order to complete such improvement. This statement may be waived by the City Council where the estimated cost of the public improvement is less than five thousand dollars ($5,000.00).

4.2. Written waivers of lien for all labor or material provided for such public improvement where public funds are involved.

5\. Any partial reduction or discharge of a surety or bond submitted in accordance with this Section to guarantee completion of public improvements shall be subject to the following restrictions:

5.1. No partial reduction or discharge approved on account of work performed and labor and materials provided shall exceed the cost of such work, labor or materials;

5.2. No partial reduction or discharge shall be approved to the extent that it would cause the balance of funds remaining to be less than one hundred ten percent (110%) of the estimated cost of work, labor, and material remaining to be performed or provided in order to complete such improvements;

5.3. A partial reduction or discharge of such financing surety may be approved upon certificate of the City Engineer; provided, however, such certificate shall not be construed as partial or complete acceptance of the improvement for which said work was performed or labor and materials provided.

6\. When the subdivider has supplied all of the documents or materials required by this Title to be submitted in support of its request for discharge of a surety or bond or acceptance of the public improvement as completed, the City Council shall approve or disapprove such request within thirty (30) days of the subdivider's application. If the subdivider's application is disapproved, a written statement of the reasons for such disapproval shall be given the subdivider. The subdivider and the City Council may mutually agree to extend the 30-day period. In addition, the 30-day period shall be extended as necessary in the event that weather prevents the City from verifying the facts contained in the subdivider's application.

### 7-1-8: REQUESTS TO WAIVE OR MODIFY SUBDIVISION REQUIREMENTS

1\. Subdivision Variances: A request to waive or modify any requirement found in Section 7-4-4 (Design Standards: Land Use) of this Title shall be processed as a variance in accordance with Chapter 3 (Administration and Enforcement) of Title 6, specifically Section 6-3-5 (Procedures) and Section 6-3-6 (Variances).

2\. Subdivision Deviations: A request to waive or modify any requirement found in this Title other than Section 7-4-4 shall be processed as a deviation as follows:

2.1. Required Review: Deviations to Title 7 shall be reviewed by the City Council, but shall not require review by the Planning and Zoning Commission. The City Council may be ordinance, grant, or grant with modification, or deny the requested deviation. The City Council may also, at its discretion, refer the proposed deviation to the Planning and Zoning Commission for its review and recommendation through a public hearing as provided in Subsection 6-3-5:4 (Procedures) of Title 6.

2.2. Notice Requirements: Except as provided in Subsection 2.2.1 below, petitioners requesting a subdivision deviation shall not be required to provide notice (e.g., publication, written notices, or a sign on the subject property).

2.2.1. Petitioners requesting a subdivision deviation for platted setbacks and building lines shall provide notice as set forth in Subsection 7-1-13 :2.4.

2.3. Standards For Subdivision Deviations: Except as otherwise provided in Section 7-1-13 (Platted Setbacks and Building Lines), any recommendation by the Planning and Zoning Commission, where required, and any decision by the City Council shall be predicated on evidence and findings that:

2.3.1. The petitioner has shown that strict adherence to the provisions of this Title would cause unnecessary hardship due to conditions which are unique to the site and not caused by the owner or petitioner; and

```text
                    2 .3.2.          The requested subdivision deviation is not contrary to the intent and
```

purpose of the provisions of this Title.

2.4. Withdrawal Of Petition: A petition for a deviation may be withdrawn by the petitioner at any time prior to its approval by the City Council. Withdrawal shall not affect the petitioner's responsibility for payment of fees under this Code.

2.5. Effective Period Of Petition: A petition for a deviation shall be valid for a period of two (2) years from the date on which the petition is filed with the Department of Transportation, Engineering, and Development.

2.5.1. Any petition for a deviation which is not processed completely in accordance with City ordinances and regulations, including the preparation and approval of required ordinances, within said two-year period, shall automatically lapse and become null and void without further action by the City.

2.5.2. The City of Naperville shall send written notice to the last known property owner of record at least thirty (30) days before any petition for a deviation shall lapse.

2.5.3. After a petition for a deviation has lapsed, all fees paid by the petitioner shall be forfeited, and any subsequent petition for a deviation shall be accompanied by the fees required by this Code.

3\. Conditions And Restrictions: The City Council may impose conditions and restrictions as may be necessary to comply with the standards set forth in this Section. If a time limit is set forth by which conditions and restrictions must be completed, they shall be completed in the time specified. If not such time limit is specified, then the conditions and restrictions shall be completed within a reasonable amount of time. The petitioner shall be obliged to fulfill and maintain all conditions and restrictions for as long as the deviation is utilized or in effect.

3.1. Such conditions and restrictions shall be reasonably conceived to fulfill public needs emanating from the proposed deviation.

3.2. Changes or alterations of conditions and restrictions shall be processed in the manner set forth in this Section for deviations.

3.3. The deviation shall be valid only if the conditions and restrictions imposed upon the premises are fulfilled.

4\. Effective Period Of Deviation: No deviation shall be valid for a period longer than two (2) years from the date of the ordinance granting the deviation unless a building permit is obtained within such period and the erection or alteration of a building or structure is started or the use is commenced within such period.

4.1. Where conditions have not substantially changed since the date on which the deviation was authorized, the owner of a parcel for which a deviation has been authorized may, prior to the expiration of said deviation, request the City Council to extend the effective period of said deviation for no more than one (1) additional period of up to two (2) years without reapplication. ### 7-1-9: CHANGES AND AMENDMENTS OF REGULATIONS

The Plan Commission may, but is not requ ired to, from time to time prepare and recommend in writing any proposed change or addition to these provisions and regulations; provided however, no public hearing before the Plan Commission or any commission or board shall be required prior to the passage of any such amendatory ordinance. ### 7-1-10: ENFORCEMENT OF PROVISIONS

The enforcing officer may call upon any department or official of the City to furnish them with such information and assistance as they may deem necessary for the observance or enforcement of this Title; and it shall be the duty of such department or officer to furnish such information and assistance whenever required. ### 7-1-11: COMPLIANCE REQUIRED FOR PERMIT ISSUANCE

1\. No building permit or occupancy permit shall be issued for any building, structure or improvement within a subdivision approved in accordance with this Title unless a valid and enforceable corporate surety bond or other financing surety in accordance with Section 7-1-5 of this Chapter is on file with the City to guarantee completion of improvements in such subdivision which the subdivider is required to install but which are not yet complete and/or accepted.

2\. Notwithstanding the provisions of Subsection 7-1-11.1 of this Section, building and occupancy permits shall be issued to individual lot owners (other than the defaulting subdivider or developer of the subdivision), even though a valid and enforceable corporate surety bond or other financing surety is not on file with the City, if the lot owner deposits cash with the City in the amount of one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of that lot's pro rata share of the estimated cost of all improvements remaining to be completed as required by this Title or an applicable development agreement, said estimated cost to be determined by the City Engineer. Such funds shall be held for use by the City for the completion of such remaining improvements. Upon completion of all such improvements by the City, a pro rata share of any remaining funds deposited under this Subsection shall be refunded to such lot owner. In the event that all such improvements are completed by the subdivider or developer as required by this Title or an applicable development agreement, all such funds shall be refunded to such lot owner.

3\. Except as provided in Subsection 5-2A-1.2 of this Code, no building permit shall be issued for the construction of any building, structure or improvement unless the owner of the land upon which said building, structure or improvement is to be constructed has complied with the requirements of Titles 5 and 9 of this Code and this Title as applied to such property. (Ord. 84-4; amd. Ord. 96-145, 8-6-1996; Ord. 00-140, 8-15-2000)

### 7-1-12: COSTS FOR PLAT REVIEW AND SUBDIVISION INSPECTION

2.1. Where a platted building or setback line is the same as the required yard for a subject property pursuant to Title 6, "Zoning Regulations", of this Code, the property owner shall only be required to seek approval of a variance to the zoning regulations and shall not be required to obtain approval of a platted setback deviation as prescribed herein.
