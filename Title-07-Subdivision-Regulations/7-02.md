# 7-2 SUBDIVISION PLAT PROCEDURES[^25]

### 7-2-1: SUBDIVISION PLAT REQUIREMENTS

1\. The petitioner shall submit a preliminary or final plat which shall show, at a minimum, on its face the following information:

1.1. All content as required by 765 ILCS 205/Plat Act as amended.

1.2. All content as required by the Transportation, Engineering and Development Business Group, as stipulated on a checklist provided by the City, as may be amended from time to time.

1.3. If a land donation is contemplated to comply with the required school or park donation, the location, acreage, and specific use of the area proposed to meet said donation shall be designated on the plat.

1.4. Any other information, as required by the Director of Transportation, Engineering and Development ("Director"), as necessary to process the requested subdivision plat.

2\. All documents, plats, supporting data, etc., and revisions thereof shall be dated and properly titled.

(Ord. No. 13-129, § 8, 11-5-2013)

### 7-2-2: SUBDIVISION PLAT PROCEDURES

1\. Initiation Of A Petition: A preliminary plat or final plat of subdivision may be proposed by an person who is the owner, lessee, or contract purchaser of the subject property. In any instance where the petitioner is a person other than the owner, the petition shall also be signed by the owner or an authorized representative of the owner.

2\. Petitions: The petitioner shall file the required petition forms, as provided by the City, and the proposed preliminary and/or final plat and all supporting documents required by the City, with the Transportation, Engineering and Development Business Group.

2.1. The preliminary and final plat may be combined, filed and approved simultaneously if all requirements hereof are met. If the preliminary plat and final plat are filed separately, the petition for the final plat approval shall be made not later than two (2) years after the preliminary plat approval has been granted. If after two (2) years from the date of the preliminary plat approval no submission of a final plat in conformance with the procedures contained herein has occurred, the preliminary plat shall automatically lapse and become null and void without further action from the City.

2.2. The Director shall promptly review the petition to determine whether it is complete. After the petition is deemed complete by the Director, the proposed plat and all supporting documents shall be transmitted to the City Council or the Planning and Zoning Commission as prescribed in Subsection 7-2-2:5 within a period of no greater than sixty (60) days from the date of the completed petition.

2.3. The Director shall provide a copy of the completed petition including the proposed plat as well as all supporting documents to each public school district and park district in which the subdivision will be located for review. The Director shall give the school and park districts twenty-one (21) days from the date on which the completed petition is transmitted to the school and park districts for submittal of written comments. If any written comments are received within the 21-day period, the Director shall forward such comments along with the proposed plat to the City Council or Planning and Zoning Commission as prescribed in Subsection 7-2-2:5 for consideration.

3\. Phasing: A petition for the final plat may be submitted in stages with each stage reflecting a portion of the approved preliminary plat which is proposed to be recorded and developed; provided, however, that such portion conforms to all requirements of these regulations, and provided further that if the development be phased, all phased final plats shall conform substantially to the preliminary plat as approved.

4\. Fees: Upon filing a petition, the petitioner shall pay the fees for processing said petition as set forth in Section 1-9F-1 of this Code, as amended from time to time.

5\. Review: Except as otherwise provided in Section 7-2-5 (Administrative Plat Procedures), the Director is hereby authorized, at their discretion, to recommend the approval of a preliminary or final subdivision plat directly to the City Council, provided that no variances, which require a public hearing before the Planning and Zoning Commission as defined in Section 6-3-5 (Procedures) of this Code, are being requested in conjunction with the subdivision plat. If deemed necessary or appropriate, the City Council may refer such plat to the Planning and Zoning Commission for its review, consideration or recommendation prior to any action by the City Council.

5.1. The City Council shall approve or deny the proposed preliminary and/or final plat within ninety (90) days of filing a completed petition, unless such time is extended by mutual consent. Even if the petitioner and the City Council do not agree upon an extension, the City Council shall be authorized to table action on the proposed plat if the City Council determines, in its sole discretion, that the petitioner has not provided all required documents or any other relevant information necessary to make a final decision on the plat. If the City council fails to take final action on a proposed plat within the 90-day period, then the passage of the 90-day period without final action on the plat shall be deemed to be a denial of the plat. The provisions of this Subsection shall, in exercise of the City's home rule authority, preempt any other provision of law or ordinance which may establish a different review duration or required approval of a plat based on inaction by the City Council, including Section 11-12-8 of the Illinois Municipal Code \[81\] (81) 65 ILCS 5/11-12-8., as amended.

5.2. The City Council may require such special conditions in the approval of the plat as it may deem necessary to ensure conformity with the intent of all comprehensive plan elements and requirements of the City ordinances.

5.3. If required by the City, an owner's acknowledgment and acceptance agreement (OAA) summarizing and including all agreements and special conditions associated with the plat shall accompany the petition for the final plat approval to be considered by the City Council.

5.4. When and if the City Council is satisfied with the final plat and the OAA, if one is required, along with all improvements, conditions, documents and financial surety hereinafter noted, pertaining to the subdivision, the City Council shall approve the final plat and the OAA and authorize and direct the signature of the plat as required by law.

6\. Notice Requirements: The City or the petitioner shall not be required to provide notice (e.g., publication, written notices, or a sign on the subject property) regarding the petition for approval of the subdivision plat sent directly to City Council.

7\. Recording Of Final Plat: The petitioner shall obtain all required signatures of the final plat as required by law and return the signed plat to the City Clerk for recording. The City Clerk shall have the final plat and OAA, if required by the City, recorded in the office of the record of DuPage or Will County, as appropriate. The original final plat after recording shall be retained on file with the office of the City Clerk.

8\. Effective Period Of Petitions:

8.1. A petition for preliminary and/or final plat approval shall be valid for a period of two (2) years from the date on which the petition is filed with the Transportation, Engineering and Development Business Group.

8.2. Any petition which is not processed completely in accordance with City ordinances and regulations within said two-year period shall automatically lapse and become null and void without further action by the City.

8.3. After a petition has lapsed, all fees paid by the petitioner shall be forfeited, and any subsequent petition shall be accompanied by the fees required by this Code.

9\. Withdrawal Of A Petition: A petition may be withdrawn by the petitioner at any time prior to its approval by City Council. Such withdrawal shall be deemed final and shall not affect the petitioner's responsibility for payment of fees required by this Code.

(Ord. No. 13-129, § 8, 11-5-2013; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)

### 7-2-3: ADD ON CONDOMINIUM PLATS

1\. Plat Requirements: Whenever additional property is being added to an add on condominium in accordance with the provisions of 765 Illinois Compiled Statutes 605/5, as now or hereafter amended, the developer or authorized officer of the condominium shall prepare a condominium plat on permanent material, tracing cloth, Mylar or equivalent so as to be suitable for recording with the county recorder of deeds at a scale of one inch equals fifty feet (1" = 50'). All revision dates shall be shown as well as the following:

1.1. Legal description of the property subject to the add on condominium;

1.2. The name, signature and seal of the registered land surveyor who prepared the plat and the date thereof;

1.3. All measurements, details and other matters as are required by Illinois Compiled Statutes to be shown on an add on condominium plat.

2\. Developer Action: Application for add on condominium plat approval shall be submitted to the Department of Community Development together with ten (10) copies of such plat.

3\. Department Of Community Development Action: The Director of community development is authorized to recommend approval of an add on condominium plat to the Mayor and City Council if the Director determines that the add on condominium plat is in substantial conformance to the terms and conditions of the final subdivision or planned unit development plat approved for such property. If the Director of the Department of Community Development determines that such add on condominium plat is not in substantial conformance to the terms and conditions of the previously approved final subdivision or planned unit development plat, the Director shall recommend disapproval of such plat, and the reasons therefor, to the Mayor and City Council.

4\. City Council Approval: After a recommendation on the add on condominium plat by the Director of the Department of Community Development, and after the City Council has satisfied itself that the proposed add on condominium plat is in accordance with the requirements of this Chapter and in substantial conformance with the terms and conditions of the previously approved final plat for such property, the City Council shall, by motion or resolution, approve such plat and authorize and direct the Mayor and City Clerk to sign and attest such plat for, and in the name of the City, and the Clerk shall attach the corporate seal thereto. If the City Council determines that such add on condominium plat is not in accordance with the requirements of this Chapter or is not in conformance with the terms and conditions of the previously approved final plat for such property, the City Council may reject such plat or require such special conditions to approval of the plat as may be necessary to ensure conformity with the requirements of this Chapter and/or the terms and conditions of the previously approved final plat for such property. Upon disapproval, a written statement setting forth the reasons for such disapproval shall be given to the developer.

5\. Recording Of Plat: The City Clerk shall cause the add on condominium plat to be recorded in the office of the recorder of DuPage and/or Will County, Illinois. Said add on condominium plat, after recording, shall be returned to the office of the City Clerk and a copy thereof shall be maintained on file. The original of the add on condominium plat shall be returned to the developer.

6\. Review Duration: When the developer has supplied all the drawings and documents required by the City ordinances to be furnished in support thereof, and if all such material meets City requirements, the City Council shall approve the proposed add on condominium plat within sixty (60) days from the date of filing application for approval of the add on condominium plat or within sixty (60) days from the date of filing the last required document, whichever is later. The applicant and the City Council may mutually agree to extend the 60-day period.

(Ord. No. 13-129, § 8, 11-5-2013)

### 7-2-4: FEES AND CHARGES

Upon filing an application for approval of a preliminary or final subdivision plat (including an add on condominium plat), the applicant shall pay the fees set forth in Section 1-9F-2 of this Code.

(Ord. No. 13-129, § 8, 11-5-2013)

### 7-2-5: ADMINISTRATIVE PLAT PROCEDURES

1\. Intent. It is the intent of this Section to permit the administrative review and approval plats of subdivision, plats of easement, and plats of dedication which meet the criteria set forth in Section 7-2-5.2 below. This Section should be administered without regard to land use or parcel size. It is not applicable to subdivision plats that establish new buildable lots or create new nonconforming lots resulting from changes in parcel size or width on the property which is the subject of the plat.

2\. Procedures. Where a proposed plat of subdivision, plat of easement, or plat of dedication is submitted on or after September 20, 2006, and includes the division of a lot, block or tract of property, which plat satisfies the following criteria, said plat shall be exempt from the procedures described in Section 7-2-2, "Subdivision Plat Procedures" of this Chapter and shall instead be processed in accordance with the administrative resubdivision process described herein.

2.1. The plat does not result in a net increase in buildable lots.

2.2. The plat does not change the existing orientation of any lot.

2.3. The plat is in compliance with the minimum zoning district requirements for lot area or lot width and subdivision ordinance requirements. Parcels that were established by deed division, assessment plat or metes and bounds description after 1989 shall be required to seek a variance if not in accordance with the post-1989 area requirements or lot width requirements.

2.4. The plat does not alter a previously platted front or corner side setback line.

2.5. Plats that were originally subdivided or parcels that were established by deed division, assessment plat, or metes and bounds description prior to 1989 that currently utilize the pre-1989 residential zoning setbacks as provided in Title 6 of this Code shall maintain the ability to utilize the aforementioned pre-1989 setbacks following subdivision of the subject property.

2.6. The plat does not require the vacation of dedicated right-of-way or right-of-way easements. Revisions to street names indicated on the subdivision plat may be administratively approved.

2.7. The plat is sought for the purpose of permitting assignment of parcel identification numbers to reflect separate ownership of dwelling units and/or property which does not increase the number of units or principal structures on any lot.

3\. Process:

3.1. Petition: Any owner of land lying within the corporate limits of the City or within the area of platting jurisdiction of the City desiring to subdivide such lands in accordance with the criteria listed in Subsection 7-2-8.2 of this Section shall submit to the City, along with a petition for development approval, a preliminary/final plat with eight (8) copies thereof. Said plat shall include the required details as described in Section 7-2-1, "Subdivision Plat Requirements" of this Chapter as applicable.

3.2. Review: Said plat shall not be required to be placed on either the agenda of the Planning and Zoning Commission or the City Council, nor shall the plat be sent to the applicable school or park districts for review. Said plat may be administratively approved by the Zoning Administrator upon determination that applicable Code requirements have been satisfied. Provided, however, that if the Zoning Administrator determines that the plat should be referred to the City Council for consideration or the petitioner for approval of said subdivision does not agree with the provisions required herein, the City Council shall make the final determination with regard to the approval of said plat.

3.3. Notification: The petitioner for approval of the subdivision shall not be required to provide notices (e.g., written notification to surrounding property owners, sign on the subject property).

3.4. Permit Issuance: Upon determination that the plat is in substantial conformance with the requirements listed in Section 7-2-1, "Subdivision Plat Requirements" of this Chapter and the petition for development approval is completed, the subject property shall be eligible for issuance of a building permit, provided that neither a temporary nor final occupancy permit shall be issued for any structure on the subject property until said plat has been properly recorded by the county in which the subject property is located.

(Ord. No. 13-129, § 8, 11-5-2013; Ord. No. 15-188, § 2, 11-17-2015; Ord. No. 17-156, § 2, 11-7-2017)

### 7-2-6: VERTICAL SUBDIVISION

1\. Defined: "Vertical subdivision" is a subdivision in which the airspace above and/or below property previously subdivided pursuant to this Title, hereinafter referenced as the "underlying subdivision plat", is divided for the purpose of sale or for the conveyance of an undivided interest coupled with the right of exclusive occupancy.

2\. Applicability:

2.1. Vertical subdivisions as provided for in this Section may be considered for new construction of a building on any one parcel or lot within the City which includes a minimum of two (2) mixed uses. For any mixed use development which includes residential condominium components, a vertical subdivision may be approved with the condition that the residential component complies with the regulations contained in the Illinois Condominium Property Act, and any other provisions applicable to residential condominiums contained in this Code.

2.2. All other provisions of this Title, as amended from time to time, shall apply to vertical subdivisions as appropriate except where more specific provisions are contained in this Chapter.

2.3. Nothing contained herein shall permit the development of any air rights separate and apart from regulations applicable to the property and the building or buildings to which the vertical subdivision plat applies, and all such development shall be subject to Title 6 of this Code.

3\. Plat Requirements:

3.1. A vertical subdivision plat shall be made by an Illinois professional land surveyor who shall prepare the plat in conformity with the standards set forth in Title 68, Chapter VII, part 1270 of the Illinois Administrative Code and shall set forth:

3.1.1. All angular and linear data along the exterior boundaries of the parcel.

3.1.2. The linear measurements and location, with reference to said exterior boundaries, of any buildings, improvements and structures located on the parcel. As to each floor that will exist in any building on the parcel, the plat shall include a three-dimensional drawing identifying the floor and include a legal description thereof.

3.1.3. The elevations at, above, or below official datum of the finished or unfinished interior surfaces of the floors and ceilings and the linear measurements of the finished or unfinished interior surfaces of the perimeter walls, and lateral extensions thereof or other monumental perimeter boundaries, where there are no wall surfaces, that part of every unit which is in any building on the parcel, and the locations of such wall surfaces or unit boundaries with respect to the exterior boundaries of the parcel projected vertically upward.

3.1.4. The elevations at, above, or below official datum and the linear measurements of the perimeter boundaries, of that part of the property which constitute a unit or a part thereof outside any building on the parcel and the location of the boundaries with respect to the exterior vertical boundaries of the parcel, projected vertically upward. Every such unit shall be identified on the plat by a distinguishing number or other symbol.

3.1.5. If the Illinois professional land surveyor does not certify that such plat accurately depicts the matters set forth in Subsections 7-2-6.3.1.3 and 7-2-6.3.1.4 of this Section, such a certification for any particular unit or units as built shall be recorded prior to the first conveyance of such particular unit or units as part of an amended plat thereby complying with the requirements of Subsections 7-2-6.3.1.3 and 7-2-6.3.1.4 of this Section. In addition to the other certificates required on the plat, the surveyor shall include a boundary survey certificate on the vertical plat of subdivision.

3.2. Each vertical subdivision plat shall assure rights of ingress and egress or access easements as appropriate.

4\. Additional Submittals:

4.1. At the time a vertical subdivision plat is submitted to the City, the subdivider shall also provide the City with documents establishing to the satisfaction of the City the legal relationship between the owners of the units and common areas described in the vertical subdivision plat and the owners of the land described in the underlying subdivision plat, and their respective responsibilities relative to ongoing governance and operation of the project.

4.2. Such documents shall include, but not be limited to, reciprocal access and easement agreements, mutual easements for utilities and structural support, a common area repair and maintenance agreement (covering common appliances and systems such as electrical, plumbing, heating, fire alarms and suppressions systems, air conditioning and other mechanical systems not located solely in a single unit), covenants and conditions affecting any units and/or lots, and any other documentation reasonably determined to be necessary or appropriate by the City.

5\. Approval Of Plat:

5.1. Plan Commission approval of a vertical subdivision plat shall not be required, but City Council approval is required.

6\. Costs For Review And Inspection:

6.1. In consideration of the expenses incurred by the City, both in professional and consulting fees, and in time expended by City employees reviewing and inspecting the proposed vertical subdivision plat, supplemental submittal documents, and the subdivision improvements, the developer or subdivider shall pay to the City a fee pursuant to a fee schedule which shall be available from the transportation, engineering and development group. Such payment shall be due prior to the recording of vertical subdivision plat by the City.

7\. Recording Of Plat:

7.1. The City Clerk shall have the vertical subdivision plat and any other related documents deemed appropriate by the City Attorney recorded in the office of the recorder of DuPage or Will County. After being recorded, the vertical subdivision plat and any other related recorded documents shall be returned to the office of the City Clerk, and a copy thereof shall be maintained on file. The original of the vertical subdivision plat shall be returned to the subdivider.

8\. Encroachments:

8.1. If, after approval of a vertical subdivision plat as provided herein, any portion of the common areas encroach upon any unit, or if any unit encroaches upon any portion of the common areas or any other unit as a result of the construction, repair, reconstruction, settlement or shifting of any building, a valid mutual easement shall exist in favor of the owner(s) of the common areas and the respective unit owners involved to the extent of the encroachment. A valid easement shall not exist in favor of any unit owner or owner of any common area who creates an encroachment by their intentional, willful or negligent conduct or that of their agent.

(Ord. No. 13-129, § 8, 11-5-2013; Ord. No. 22-044, § 2, 5-3-2022)
