# 7-5 SUBDIVISION NAMES

### 7-5-1: PROHIBITION OF SIMILAR NAMES

No plat of subdivision, resubdivision, assessment, planned unit development or plat of survey under the Condominium Act[^28] shall be valid nor entitled to record if the record name or part thereof of said subdivision, resubdivision, assessment, planned unit development or plat of survey under the Condominium Act is the same as, duplicates, closely approximates or is similar to or pronounced the same as the name of any previously approved subdivision within the subdivision jurisdiction of the City.

(Ord. 85-226, 12-2-85)

### 7-5-2: APPLICABILITY

A subdivision name or part thereof shall be considered as duplicating, closely approximating, similar to or pronounced the same as the name of an existing subdivision name if it contains a proper, historical, geographical, locational, mythological, famous, fictitious or personal name, words, or combination thereof which is the same as, similar to or pronounced the same as a word in the name of any other previously approved subdivision within the subdivision jurisdiction of the City. Common nouns such as valley, creek, forest, etc., may be used freely as part of a subdivision name except to the extent that such nouns, when used in combination with other words, result in a subdivision name which duplicates, closely approximates, is similar to or pronounced the same as the name of a previously approved subdivision.

(Ord. 85-226, 12-2-85)

### 7-5-3: EXCEPTIONS

Notwithstanding the provisions set forth in this Chapter, a subdivision may have a name which, in combination with other words, uses a proper, historical, geographical, locational, mythological, famous, fictitious or personal name or word used in the name of a previously approved subdivision if the name of such subdivision is not the same as or pronounced the same as the name of a previously approved subdivision, provided one of the following criteria is also met:

1\. The proposed subdivision is owned or developed by the same person who owned, subdivided or developed the previously approved subdivision and is also contiguous to the previously approved subdivision.

2\. The proposed subdivision is contiguous to the previously approved subdivision and the person who is platting such subdivision files a written consent with the City to the use of such similar name of the person who platted the previously approved subdivision.

For the purposes of this Chapter, a proposed subdivision shall be considered contiguous to a previously approved subdivision notwithstanding the separation of said subdivisions by a right-of-way.

(Ord. 85-226, 12-2-85)

### 7-5-4: NAME CHANGES

No person may change the name of a previously approved subdivision for any purpose without prior approval of the City Council subject to the regulations set forth in this Chapter for naming a proposed subdivision.

(Ord. 85-226, 12-2-85)

### 7-5-5: SIGNS

Subject to the provisions of Chapter 4 (Street Graphics Control) of Title 5 (Building Regulations) of this Code, no sign or street graphic shall be constructed or erected which identifies or advertises a previously approved subdivision by any name other than the name displayed on the plat of subdivision, resubdivision, assessment, planned unit development or plat of survey under the Condominium Act or other name approved in accordance with the provisions of this Chapter. This Section shall not be construed to require the inclusion of alphabetical, numerical or alphanumerical identification of a portion of a subdivision platted in phases or units.

(Ord. 85-226, 12-2-85)

### 7-5-6: NONCONFORMING SUBDIVISION NAMES

1\. A subdivision name which is prohibited by or is in violation of this Chapter may be continued for identification, advertising or other lawful purposes if such name is displayed on a plat of subdivision, resubdivision, assessment, planned unit development or plat of survey under the Condominium Act approved by the City Council for record prior to the effective date of this Chapter.

2\. A subdivision name which is prohibited by or is in violation of this Chapter may be continued for identification, advertising or other lawful purposes, even though such name is not displayed on a recorded plat of subdivision, resubdivision, assessment, planned unit development or plat of survey under the Condominium Act, if such name was in common usage prior to the effective date of this Chapter for identification and advertising of such subdivision to the public and changing such name would cause a substantial hardship to the owners or developers of such subdivision or the public.

(Ord. 85-226, 12-2-85)
