# Ordinance No. 85-10

AN ORDINANCE ADOPTING THE MUNICIPAL  
CODE OF NAPERVILLE OF 1984

WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of Naperville has undertaken a program which has resulted in the production of a total recodification of the City's ordinances and the creation of a new comprehensive code of ordinances of the City of Naperville; and

WHEREAS, it is the intent of the City Council that all existing City ordinances shall be amended by portions of the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984 with which they are in conflict, but shall remain in full force and effect to the extent not in conflict with the provisions of the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984; and

WHEREAS, it is further the intent of the City Council that all existing City ordinances passed since January 1, 1984 which amended either the text of the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1960, as amended, or the text of Ordinance 80-5 of the City, as amended, being the zoning ordinance of the City of Naperville, shall remain in full force and effect and be codified into the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984 by later supplement thereof to the extent not previously incorporated into the text thereof;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF NAPERVILLE, DU PAGE AND WILL COUNTIES, ILLINOIS, in the exercise of its home rule powers, as follows:

SECTION 1: That the following titles are hereby adopted within the comprehensive Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984 and shall constitute the ordinances of the City of Naperville:

| Title 1  | Administrative                   |
|----------|----------------------------------|
| Title 2  | Boards and Commissions           |
| Title 3  | Business and License Regulations |
| Title 4  | Health and Sanitation            |
| Title 5  | Building Regulations             |
| Title 6  | Zoning Regulations               |
| Title 7  | Subdivision Regulations          |
| Title 8  | Public Utilities                 |
| Title 9  | Public Ways and Property         |
| Title 10 | Police Regulations               |
| Title 11 | Motor Vehicles and Traffic       |

These titles are appended to and made a part of this Ordinance.

SECTION 2: All existing ordinances of the City of Naperville passed since January 1, 1984 which amended either the text of the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1960, as amended, or the text of Ordinance No. 80-5 of the City of Naperville, as amended, being the zoning ordinance of the City of Naperville, shall remain in full force and effect and be codified into the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984 by later supplement thereof.

SECTION 3: Except as provided in Section 2 of this Ordinance, any previously passed ordinance of the City of Naperville that is in conflict with the provisions contained within the aforementioned titles of the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984 adopted in Section 1 of this Ordinance shall, to the extent of that conflict, be repealed or amended.

SECTION 4: This Ordinance and each of its terms shall be the effective legislative act of a Home Rule Municipality without regard to whether such ordinance should (a) contain any terms contrary to the provisions of current or subsequent non-preemptive state law; or, (b) legislate in a manner or regarding a matter not delegated to municipalities by state law. It is the intent of the City Council of the City of Naperville that to the extent that the terms of this Ordinance should be inconsistent with non-preemptive state law, that this Ordinance shall supersede state law in that regard within its jurisdiction.

SECTION 5: If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase or portion of this Ordinance of the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984 is for any reason held invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction, such portion shall be deemed a separate, severable, distinct and independent provision and such holding shall not affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance or the Municipal Code of Naperville of 1984.

SECTION 6: This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage, approval and publication in pamphlet form as provided by law.

PASSED This 4th day of February, 1985.  
AYES: Mayor Price, Councilmen Newkirk, Phelan, Rasmussen and Wehrli
